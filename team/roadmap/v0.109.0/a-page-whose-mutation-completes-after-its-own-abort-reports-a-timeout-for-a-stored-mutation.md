# A page whose mutation completes after its own abort reports a timeout for a stored mutation

Status: built on 2026-10-21 on the seat's branch; the section Built 2026-10-21 holds the evidence; it closes at the GA as shipped.

Record before the build: carried into v0.109.0 at the v0.108.0 GA on 2026-10-21, still raised for a decision.

Record before the release: raised at the v0.107.0 cut on 2026-10-17 by the lead from a finding outside that version's items, recorded in the round's decisions as the ledger's entry; written down with the repair's shape; owner by lane at the next version's launch.

## What was seen

The page aborts a mutation request after 10 s and reports a timeout to its user. The server's worker that stores the mutation keeps running past the abort and, from v0.107.0, announces the stored mutation from the worker, so the mutation lands and its frame reaches the page while the page's report says it timed out (the runtime seat's reading of the server at the announcement item's design, 2026-10-16; not read on the page).

## Desired contract

A page never tells its user a mutation timed out when the server stored and announced it; the report says what is known at the abort and the list shows the stored result when its frame arrives.

## What to do

The page treats a frame that names its own aborted request's target, arriving after the abort, as that request's answer; or its timeout report says the mutation may have completed and the list is refreshed.

## Acceptance

1. A page reading in a browser: a mutation held past 10 s with the server's hold seam, the report observed, then the frame's arrival and what the page shows.
2. A store-level pin for the chosen shape, red first.
3. The design text.

## Built 2026-10-21

The page takes the row's second shape. A mutation the server stores and announces goes through `requestMutation` (`api/transport.ts`), which `mutate` in `api/client.ts` calls for ten of them: an entry's create, delete, move and transfer, a draft's create, a diagram's create, a draft's discard and promotion, a team config's write and a session conflict's overwrite. At the answer deadline it ends with `UncertainOutcomeError` (`api/errors.ts`) where any other request keeps the plain timeout, the POSTs that only read (`inspectDraft`, `draftTerminalPaths`, `readTeamConfig`, the reply acknowledgments) among them. Each reporting caller passes the error to `reportUncertainOutcome` (`state/mutationReport.ts`) under its own action words: an uncertain outcome gives "<action> may have completed: no answer within 10 s" and lists the affected view at once, and any other error keeps the caller's failure line as it was. The watch frame that announces a stored result lists the view again through the frame handlers, which this change leaves alone; no frame is taken as a request's answer, so a draft whose create got no answer is listed and opens no tab, and nothing is retried.

The callers, their words and what they list: "New draft" (the command and a draft staged in Hybrid Nav), "New diagram" (staged and from its command), "New slide deck", "New Rich Prompt draft" (the card's alert, with its Retry) and "Reopen draft" list the drafts; "Draft close" (a discard) and "Draft save" (a promotion) list the drafts and the tree and keep the tab open, as a failure does; "Discarding <name>" (a broken draft from the warnings dialog) lists the drafts and reads the workspace's warnings again; "create" (a new file, folder, or file or folder), "duplicate", "rename", "move" (one entry or several), "paste" and "delete" list the tree; a delete of several entries counts an uncertain one with the refused, names it as it names the first refusal, keeps it selected, and lists the tree. A team config's write stops the bootstrap before any spawn, the dialog reads "bootstrap stopped: saving the team config may have completed: no answer within 10 s", the tree is listed and the form stays as typed, since the stored config is the older one whenever the write did not land. "Overwrite" and "Keeping your version" read nothing: the conflict bar is `tab.diskConflicted`, which the document session sets from its own `conflict` frame, the server's announcement of the overwrite. No caller rolls back optimistic state on a failure, so none has a rollback to skip: what each keeps on a failure it keeps on an uncertain outcome (an open draft tab, the tabs of a moved entry on their old path, an uncertain delete's path in the selection). Outside the ten: the terminal's discard of a closed terminal's Rich Prompt draft (`void api.discardDraft`) and the silent discard of an empty file on close report nothing and are unchanged; the upload's cancel is upslot's page half, which adds its sentences to `state/mutationReport.ts`.

The design text is the section "A mutation with no answer" in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/design.md`.
