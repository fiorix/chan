# The status bar's broken-draft warning outlives the condition it reports

Status: built on 2026-10-22 on the seat's branch; the section Built 2026-10-22 holds the evidence; it closes at the GA as shipped.

Record before the build: carried into v0.109.0 at the v0.108.0 GA on 2026-10-21, still raised for a decision.

Record before the release: raised at the v0.107.0 cut on 2026-10-17 by the lead from a finding outside that version's items, recorded in the round's decisions as the ledger's entry; written down with the repair's shape; owner by lane at the next version's launch.

## What was seen

The status bar's workspace warnings come from the workspace payload; a `draft` frame runs the drafts refresh only. A window that discards a truly broken draft refreshes its own workspace; another window of the same workspace receives the discarded frame, updates its drafts group, and keeps the broken-draft warning in its status bar until its next workspace refresh (the review seat's read at source of the draft-creation item's acceptance 2, 2026-10-16; the frontend seat's reading at the base saw the warning outlive a completed creation the same way).

## Desired contract

The status bar's broken-draft warning leaves when the draft it names is discarded or made whole, in every window of the workspace, without waiting for an unrelated refresh.

## What to do

The draft frame refreshes the workspace payload as well, or the status bar's broken entries follow the drafts list.

## Acceptance

1. A pin on a second window, or a store test feeding the frame, red first at the base.
2. The design text of the status bar's warnings.

## Lead's rulings 2026-10-22

The lead's task 25 records these rulings in its words:

- S1, the composition, inside `surfaceWorkspaceWarnings` (D1). `activeWorkspaceWarnings(info)` becomes one composition over `workspace.info` and `drafts`: once `drafts.loaded`, the payload's `broken_draft` and `draft_preflight_failed` entries are dropped and `drafts.broken` and `drafts.preflight` take their place; every other kind stays the payload's (today none; D2 names the two kinds so "other kinds" has one reading); until the first successful list, the payload's entries stand; the session dismissal set applies to the composed set (the keys are the same across sources). All five surface sites take it unchanged: boot, `refreshWorkspace` (every file-event burst reaches it), discard, dismiss, and the new `onDraftsListed` listener that surfaces after each answered list. No workspace request is added on a draft frame.
- S2. The list's extra broken-row entries (a row whose id, pin or inspection fails; `source.root` is `draft`, so the dialog discards them as the others) are in the status bar's set; the row's closing section says so in one sentence.
- S3. "Made whole" is a draft made whole through the server, whose routes emit a `draft` frame: `created` (`routes/drafts.rs` 418 and 459), `modified` (`routes/files.rs` 2656 and `routes/attachments.rs` 226, an attachment into a draft, D5), `promoted` (591), `discarded` (563). A repair in the draft store outside the server emits none and is outside the contract; its warning leaves at the window's next list; the row's closing section states the limit. No server change.
- S4. `resyncDrafts()` lists when the window shows a draft warning as well (a `workspace-warnings` status action or an open dialog), with or without a draft tab or an earlier list.
- S5 with D3. `discardWorkspaceWarning`'s success path lists drafts (one list per discard) instead of reading `/api/workspace`, and the dialog's close or its refusal notice follows that list; `DraftsGroup`'s own `refreshDrafts()` after a discard goes. A failed list keeps the last successful set (`drafts.loaded` stays true; the rows stay). Optional, if it costs nothing: drop the discarded key locally once its discard succeeded.
- S7, the design text, the sentence in Review109's amended words, present tense, in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/design.md` beside the status bar's or the drafts' paragraph: "Workspace warning rows for broken drafts and an unavailable draft store follow the workspace payload until a workspace window's first successful drafts list and that window's latest successful list after it; draft frames refresh that list and reconcile the status pill and warnings dialog, while other workspace warnings follow the workspace payload."

## Built 2026-10-22

`state/store.svelte.ts` composes the warning set in `activeWorkspaceWarnings`, inside the shared `surfaceWorkspaceWarnings` path: the workspace payload supplies warnings before the first successful drafts list, then the list supplies `broken_draft` and `draft_preflight_failed` while every other kind stays with the payload. The same session dismissal keys filter both sources. A module-lifetime `onDraftsListed` listener reconciles the status action and open dialog after a successful answer; boot, workspace refresh, dismiss and discard use the same composition. `resyncDrafts` also lists for a warning status action or open dialog, without requiring a draft tab or an earlier successful list. Local discard awaits the list and makes no workspace request, and `components/DraftsGroup.svelte` shares that refresh instead of asking again. Failed lists keep the latest successful rows and warning set; a discard followed by a failed list can leave the discarded row visible until a later successful list. The optional removal of that key and the optional suppression of unchanged-set status updates were not taken.

The list's extra broken-row warnings for failed id, pin or inspection enter the status bar's set, including their draft source for discard. The six server emit sites are `routes/drafts.rs`'s two `created` calls, its `discarded` and `promoted` calls, `routes/files.rs`'s `modified` call and `routes/attachments.rs`'s `modified` call for an attachment into a draft. No server code changes. A repair outside the server emits none of those frames and is outside the immediate-update contract; its warning leaves at that window's next successful list.
