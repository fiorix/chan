## What is there now

Read at `f453b0b2e`, by reading; not run. Paths are under `web/packages/workspace-app/src/`.

- The shared guard. `refocusRefused(slot)` (`state/focusGuard.ts:23-25`) refuses a refocus while a survey covers the terminal in `slot`, while the Computers launcher is shown over the window, or while `modalDialogOpen()` holds (`state/store.svelte.ts:5413-5426`: the prompt, path prompt, confirm, draft close, conflict, workspace warnings and contacts import dialogs, the Team dialog, the desktop close prompt and the window-wide survey). The terminal's own refocus goes through it (`components/TerminalTab.svelte:261-264`), and so do the command launcher's focus return (`components/CommandLauncher.svelte:855`) and the pane tree's (`components/PaneTreeOverlay.svelte:173`).
- The launcher's mark is a DOM attribute, not state: `launcherShownOverWindow()` reads `document.documentElement.hasAttribute(LAUNCHER_SHOWN_ATTR)` (`state/focusGuard.ts:9`, `:15-17`), so nothing that reads the guard is re-run when the launcher hides. The window tab bar turns the same attribute into state with a `MutationObserver` (`components/WindowTabBar.svelte:191-203`).
- Overlays are not owners. The guard does not read `topOverlay()`; `keyboardOwnedAbovePanes()` (`state/store.svelte.ts:5396-5398`) names the command launcher, Search and Settings as owners, for the pane chords only.
- The Rich Prompt reads no guard. A focused terminal's refocus yields to its open composer (`components/TerminalTab.svelte:693-699`), so the composer is the surface that takes focus, and it takes it whoever holds the keyboard: the delivered and drained phases (`components/RichPrompt.svelte:444`, `focusAt(0)` in a microtask, gated on `focused` only); a recall (`:450`, `focusEnd()`); the tab-focus pulse effect (`:481-488`); the exported `focus()` (`:492-494`), which its terminal calls to give the keyboard back after a move (`components/TerminalTab.svelte:792`); a submit (`:661`, `view.focus()` in `sendSubmitted`, which a submit that waits for its images' paths runs up to 5 s later, `:174`, `:624-641`); and the mount of both editors, which take `autoFocus={focused}` (`:951`, `:963`). The editors also take focus on each change of their text from the composer while `autoFocus` holds (`editor/Wysiwyg.svelte:833-835` and `editor/Source.svelte:433-435` pass it to `sync.applyExternal`, which focuses at `editor/base.ts:306`), so a delivery that empties the composer focuses it by that path as well.
- The milestone review's scenario: terminal A's composer holds a queued prompt while its agent is busy; a survey over A, or a window-wide one, shows and its card has the keyboard; A goes idle, the prompt drains and `prompt-delivered` arrives; the composer takes the keyboard, and the `1` meant for the survey is typed into the draft. The same holds under the Team dialog, the desktop close prompt and the Computers launcher, and, since overlays are not owners, under the command launcher, Search and Settings.
- Only `ModalShell` dialogs pull stray focus back (`components/modalStack.ts:44-56`, with `repair` at `:25-32`): the confirm, prompt, path prompt, draft close, conflict, contacts import and workspace warnings dialogs. The Team dialog (`components/TeamDialog.svelte:352`), the desktop close prompt (`components/CloseConfirmOverlay.svelte:150`) and the survey overlay (`components/BubbleOverlay.svelte:137`) have no such repair.
- The editors' delayed focus decides when it is scheduled, not when it runs: `if (autoFocus) requestAnimationFrame(...)`, then `view.focus()` in the frame, after the mount (`editor/Wysiwyg.svelte:774-779`, `editor/Source.svelte:325-330`), after the first caret restore (`editor/Wysiwyg.svelte:811-816`, `editor/Source.svelte:423-428`) and in `resetCaret` (`editor/Wysiwyg.svelte:534-539`, `editor/Source.svelte:213-218`). An owner that takes the keyboard before the frame runs loses it once. The branch's review adds that a hidden page holds its frames until it shows, so a survey that lands meanwhile loses the keyboard when the window comes back.
- The survey overlay's own return. When its survey closes, the overlay focuses the element it remembered when the survey opened, which can be the composer (`components/BubbleOverlay.svelte:62-73`, remembered at `:84-94`). It reads `ownsKeyboard()` but not the guard, so a survey that closes under the Team dialog or the Computers launcher moves focus into the composer.
- A survey card after its terminal moves. The terminal remembers the find bar, the composer or the terminal itself as the surface to give the keyboard back to after a move (`components/TerminalTab.svelte:362-364`, `:769-774`, `:779-795`), never a survey card. The overlay's focus effect reads its survey, its pane's ownership and, over a terminal, the pulse (`components/BubbleOverlay.svelte:56-61`), not the terminal's moves. So when closing a pane moves a terminal with a survey over it, the keyboard goes to the composer if the composer held it before the survey showed, through the unguarded `focus()`, or stays on the page body when `focusTerminal` refuses; nothing hands it back to the card, and keys go nowhere until the user clicks it.
- The tab-focus pulse blurs whatever holds focus. `bumpTabFocusPulse()` (`state/tabs.svelte.ts:3951-3965`) bumps the pulse and blurs `document.activeElement` unless it is the body. A `ModalShell` repairs that; the Team dialog, the desktop close prompt and a window-wide survey, whose focus effect does not read the pulse (`components/BubbleOverlay.svelte:59-61`), do not, so a pulse fired while one is open drops its field to the body. Which user actions fire a pulse while such a dialog is open is not established.

## Desired contract

1. One rule says who owns the keyboard over a terminal and its composer: a modal dialog, whether a `ModalShell` or not (the prompt, path prompt, confirm, draft close, conflict, workspace warnings and contacts import dialogs, the Team dialog and the desktop close prompt); a survey over that terminal, and a window-wide survey; the Computers launcher while it is shown over the window; and an overlay over the panes (the command launcher, Search, Settings). While one of them owns the keyboard, neither the terminal nor its composer takes focus by itself.
2. The rule is reactive. Each owner's showing and hiding is state, the Computers launcher's mark included, so a derived value or an effect that read the rule while an owner showed reads it again when the owner lets go.
3. Every path that moves focus into the composer reads the rule at the moment it acts: the delivered and drained phases, a recall, the tab-focus pulse and the exported `focus()`, the editors' mount and the swap between the source and rendered editors, the editors' focus on a change of their text from the composer, and a submit sent after it waited for its images' paths. The editors' delayed focus reads it inside the animation frame, not when it asks for the frame.
4. The survey overlay's return at its close reads the rule: the element it remembered gets the keyboard only while no other owner holds it. A survey over a terminal that moves gets the keyboard back after the move, as the find bar, the composer and the terminal do.
5. A tab-focus pulse leaves focus where it is while a dialog or a survey holds it, a `ModalShell` or not.
6. An owner's own closing hands the keyboard back as it does today: an owner does not count against the focus return of its own closing (the command launcher's return reads the same rule).
7. Under an owner, each phase leaves the composer's text and caret as it does with no owner; a recall puts the caret at the end of the text that came back. Once the owner lets go, the next pulse gives the composer the keyboard, and an editor that mounts afterwards, as after a swap between source and rendered, comes up with it. A background tab's composer takes no focus, and a submit from the keymap or a strip button leaves the composer holding the keyboard, as today.

When the survey's pane is not the focused pane it releases a focused descendant of the card only `if (focusedSurveyId && !eligible && card?.contains(document.activeElement))` (`:79-82`). A survey that appeared while its pane was unfocused never set `focusedSurveyId`, so a descendant that gained the keyboard by another path is never released.
- The card's option, follow-up and dismiss buttons are plain `<button>`s (`:160-192`), in the page's sequential focus order whatever pane is focused; the card itself has `tabindex="-1"` (`:146`). A Shift+Tab or Tab from the focused pane's element walks into them. No press happens, so `setActivePane` does not run: the pane runs it on `mousedown` only (`components/Pane.svelte:1515-1518`), and no `focusin` handler sets the active pane (by grep).
- The card's keydown answers 1 to 9, F and X or Escape whenever the event reaches the card (`:109-130`, `onCardKeydown`), without reading `ownsKeyboard()` (`:43-47`). So with pane B focused and a survey over pane A's terminal, a Shift+Tab into A's last option button and a bare `1` answers A's survey while `activePaneId` is B, the focus ring visible on the button. Every pointer path sets the active pane first (`Pane.svelte:1515-1522`), so only keyboard navigation reaches this.
- The survey row's tests model Tab in jsdom (`components/Pane.browserFocus.test.ts:233-257`) and pin that another pane's pulse and a typed `1` leave the browser's keyboard alone; none walks focus into the card.

The options as drafted:

- (a) Buttons leave the Tab order while the pane is unfocused, plus the key guard and the release (recommended: closes the only path in, inside the existing model where sequential focus never moves the active pane).
