# Secret masking does nothing under the ghostty backend

Status: accepted for v0.116.0 by the owner's word of 2026-10-30; built on `v0116/ghostty-mask` the same day; the macOS desktop read by eye is open.

## What was asked

The owner's words of 2026-10-30: "is it still not possible to support the secret masking in the ui for ghostty, like we do for xterm?", and after a read of what it would take: "i want you to put the secret masking for ghostty in a roadmap item for the next version, not for now..".

## Evidence

Read at `cff4cb0a1` on `desktop/terminal-prototypes`; the masking code is the same on `main` at `a4b5844b9`.

- The xterm masker (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/terminal/secretMasking.ts`, `TerminalSecretMasker`) reads buffer lines through `translateToString`, `getCell(x).getWidth()` / `getChars()` and `isWrapped`, finds `NAME=value` values with `SecretAssignmentMatcher` and `findSecretMaskSpans`, and covers each span with an xterm decoration anchored to a marker (`registerMarker`, `registerDecoration`, the decoration's `onRender`). The buffer stays copyable.
- `TerminalTab.svelte` builds the masker only when the backend is xterm (`if (backend === "xterm") secretMasker = new TerminalSecretMasker(...)`). Under ghostty the per-tab menu toggle and the `terminal.secret_masking` preference mask nothing, and the Settings hints say masking is xterm-only.
- `terminalBackendFromPrefs` (`terminal/backend.ts`) puts a control terminal whose masking starts on on xterm whatever the ghostty preference says, so a control window never runs ghostty.
- ghostty-web `0.4.0-next.20.g1858a59` (`dist/index.d.ts`) has the same line interface (`buffer.active.getLine(y)` with `translateToString`, `isWrapped` and `getCell`), `viewportY`, `onRender`, `onScroll` and the renderer's cell metrics, and no `registerMarker` or `registerDecoration`.
- Chan scrolls the ghostty viewport by fractional rows on macOS (`GhosttyViewportController.handleWheel` calls `scrollLines` with a fraction), so anything drawn over ghostty's canvas has to follow a fractional row offset.

## Desired contract

With masking on, a ghostty terminal masks the values an xterm terminal masks, in the same colour, with the buffer still copyable and selection unchanged. The per-tab menu toggle, the preference and the control-window default behave as they do under xterm, and a control terminal whose masking starts on runs whichever backend the ghostty preference picks.

## What to do

- Split `secretMasking.ts` into backend-neutral detection (the matcher and `findSecretMaskSpans`, typed against a minimal line interface both buffers satisfy) and two painters: the existing xterm decorations and a ghostty layer.
- The ghostty layer is a pointer-transparent overlay over ghostty's canvas, repainted at most once per frame on `onRender`, `onScroll` and resize from a scan of the visible rows and the rows they wrap with. With no markers to track lines, each repaint rescans what is visible. Spans are placed from the renderer's cell metrics, the host padding and the fractional viewport offset.
- Drop the ghostty carve-outs: the forced xterm in `terminalBackendFromPrefs`, the Settings hints that call masking xterm-only, and any doc that says the same.
- Keep the masker's self-disable path: a failure turns masking off for that terminal and says so.

## Acceptance

- Unit tests drive detection and the ghostty layer against a fake ghostty buffer: a masked `NAME=value` line, a value that wraps, a viewport scrolled by whole and fractional rows, the alternate screen, and masking toggled off.
- In the macOS desktop, read by eye: a `FOO_TOKEN=...` line is masked under ghostty as under xterm, stays masked through output, a trackpad scroll through the scrollback and a resize, and copies unmasked; with the ghostty preference on, a control terminal opens on ghostty with masking on.
- svelte-check and the full vitest suite green; browser checks 93 (`terminal-secret-masking`) and 94 (`terminal-ghostty-toggle`) green, extended to cover masking under ghostty.

## Built on `v0116/ghostty-mask`, 2026-10-30

- `terminal/secretMasking.ts` is the backend-neutral detection: the suffix matcher, the launch-URL token rule and `findSecretMaskSpans`, typed against a `MaskLine` both buffers satisfy. `secretMaskingXterm.ts` holds the xterm painter (`TerminalSecretMasker`, the decorations and markers as before). `secretMaskingGhostty.ts` holds the ghostty painter, `GhosttySecretMasker`.
- The ghostty painter is not the overlay element the item sketched. Read at the pinned ghostty-web (0.4.0-next.20.g1858a59): `onRender` is declared but never fired (the emitter is constructed and disposed and nothing calls fire), and the renderer paints every animation frame on its own loop. The painter therefore wraps the renderer's `render` (the guarded private-hook pattern of `ghosttyCompat.ts`) and fills each span onto the canvas after the frame's content, so the mask is on screen in the frame that draws the value, with no element to lag a frame behind a scroll. The visible rows and the rows they wrap with are rescanned only when the viewport, the grid, the buffer type or length, or a row the parser marked dirty changed (the wasm's `isRowDirty` and `needsFullRedraw`, read before the original render clears them); an idle frame repaints the cached spans. Ghostty draws visible row r at r times the cell height whatever the fraction of the viewport offset, so a fractional offset maps rows as its floor does, and the painter maps the same way. Writes reach the painter through the dirty rows, so the write hooks are no-ops; scanAll and scanViewport force a rescan at the next frame; a toggle off redraws the covered rows; a hook that is missing, a read that throws or a paint that throws disables masking, logs, and reports through the same status as the xterm masker ("Secret masking stopped working in this terminal").
- Wrap flags: a screen row carries the parser's flag; a scrollback line keeps none in the pinned build (every scrollback line reads unwrapped, measured), so a scrollback line filled to its last column is read as wrapping into the next. A full hard-ended line is read the same way, and its cost is a mask that can reach into the next line's first word; the cost of the opposite reading is a wrapped value's continuation in clear. A group reaches at most a screenful above and below the viewport.
- The carve-outs are gone: `terminalBackendFromPrefs` takes the preferences alone, a control terminal spawns on whichever backend `terminal.ghostty` picks with masking on, the tab menu row reads "Secret masking: on/off" on both backends and the toggle flips either painter, the Settings hints and the config reference say masking works on both backends, and `design.md` describes the two painters.
- Tests: `secretMaskingGhostty.test.ts` drives the hook the way the render loop does over a fake buffer (a masked line, a value wrapped onto a screen row, a viewport scrolled by whole and fractional rows into the scrollback, a full scrollback line read as wrapping and a short one not, the screenful bound, the alternate screen, an idle frame reading nothing, dirty rows and forced frames reading again, the toggle off redrawing and painting nothing, a colour change, a renderer without its context, a throwing read, a throwing paint, dispose) and over the pinned wasm terminal and real `CanvasRenderer` on the software canvas with the pixels read back (a value, a wrapped value on screen and again from the scrollback, a 2.5-row scroll, the alternate screen and the toggle). The mounted `TerminalTab.ghosttyBackend.test.ts` pins the hook on the renderer, the toggle in place, the control-window matrix on ghostty and the failure status; `backend.test.ts` pins that masking never moves a terminal off its backend; the Settings tests pin the new hint. The software canvas moved to `__tests__/softwareCanvas.ts`, shared with `ghosttyCompat.test.ts`.
- Browser check 93's ghostty leg now proves masking rather than its absence: with `terminal.ghostty` and `terminal.secret_masking` on, a new terminal mounts the canvas and no xterm DOM, the canvas pixels read back through `getImageData` hold exactly two opaque rectangles of the mask colour (runs of mask pixels merged down the rows, each at least a cell high), one per value on its own row, 13 and 7 cells wide, no `.terminal-secret-mask` element exists, a trusted drag over the rows and the copy chord yield the cleartext, the per-tab toggle clears the rectangles in place and a second dispatch paints them again. The measured rectangles on this host: `{x: 72, y: 21, w: 104, h: 21}` after `GH_TOKEN=` (9 cells) and `{x: 112, y: 42, w: 56, h: 21}` after `QUOTED_SECRET=` (14 cells), at 8 by 21 px cells. The leg's own toggle is its negative control: the same reader finds 0 rectangles with masking off and 2 again with it on.
- Checks on the host: svelte-check clean; vitest green for the terminal masking, backend, compat, replay, mounted terminal, settings and window-mode suites (19 new tests, 233 in the touched files); the browser suite's Node lib tests (55); after `make web`, `cargo build -p chan` and the echo extension, browser checks 93 (`terminal-secret-masking`) and 94 (`terminal-ghostty-toggle`) PASS alone (`SMOKE_ONLY=93-,94-`, 17.4 s and 23.2 s); `make web-check` and `make pre-push` as recorded in the commit.
