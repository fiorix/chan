# A tmux pane drag starts a fight between chan and tmux

Status: raised by the owner on 2026-11-01, accepted for v0.120.1 the same day, built; the patch cut closes it.

## What was asked

The owner's observation after v0.120.0: dragging the divider between two tmux panes in chan moves the pane, tmux moves it back, and from there the two keep moving back and forth.

## What is there

Read at `c43b29433` (the v0.120.0 GA):

- The drag: `web/packages/workspace-app/src/components/Workspace.svelte` (`startResize`) previews the ratio locally on mousemove and sends one `resize-pane` on mouseup through `api.tmuxRequest` (`POST /api/terminals/<session>/tmux-request`), resolved by `tmuxResizeForSplit` in `state/tmuxResize.ts`; Hybrid Nav's keyboard resize sends the same from `App.svelte`.
- The layout adoption: the `tmux_layout` window command in `state/store.svelte.ts` calls `setTmuxLayout(frame.layout)` and then `reconcileLayout(tmuxLayoutToSer(frame.layout))`, and `state/tmuxLayout.ts` derives each split's ratio from tmux's pane sizes.
- The window-size effect in `App.svelte` reads `tmuxWindowGrid()` from `state/tmuxWindowSize.svelte.ts`: the fold of every pane's host grid (`tab.tmuxHostGrid`, the cells each pane's own element can show at its current size, measured by `measureTmuxHost` in `components/TerminalTab.svelte` through `fit.proposeDimensions()` and kept by `setTerminalTmuxHostGrid` in `state/tabs.svelte.ts`) over the layout tree, one cell per divider, debounced 150 ms, keyed by `session:colsxrows`, sent as `{ request: "window-size", pane, cols, rows }`; `crates/chan-tmux/src/command.rs` renders it as `refresh-client -C <window>:<cols>x<rows>`.
- The server submits a `resize-pane` with no follow-up query (`request_resize_pane`, `Purpose::Fire`, in `crates/chan-library/src/terminal_sessions/tmux_connection.rs`): the layout that answers a drag is tmux's `%layout-change` alone.
- The fight: host grids change with every split ratio, so the drag's preview, the layout tmux returns for the resize, and each layout chan adopts all move the folded window grid by a cell or two (the v0.120.0 row's reading saw 173, 174 and 176 across two drags). Each `window-size` makes tmux resize the whole window and redistribute the panes proportionally, which undoes part of the pane resize; chan adopts that layout, the fold moves again, another `window-size` follows, and the two keep correcting each other.
- The mounted test written against that base recorded it: one drag over a 121 by 40 cell window sent `window-size` 141 by 40 during the preview, the `resize-pane` for 83 cells, then `window-size` 169 by 40 and 167 by 40 as the hosts re-measured after tmux's answer; a layout adopted with no drag sent `window-size` 161 by 40; a change of the pane tree's own box sent nothing, since nothing watched it.

## What changed

- The value stays the hosts' fold: `tmuxWindowGrid()` folds each pane's host grid (`tab.tmuxHostGrid`, what its own element can show inside the pane margins, the dividers and the renderer's insets, as `measureTmuxHost` proposes it) over the last layout, one cell per divider, exactly as before. It is the fold and not the pane tree's area in cells because of the pane chrome: at the reading's 1400 by 900 box the area holds 175 by 47 cells while the hosts show 168 by 44, and a window sized to the area runs every pane about three columns and a row past its host, clipping a prompt or a status line.
- The trigger is the pane tree's area alone. `App.svelte` watches the `main` element around `<Workspace />` with a `ResizeObserver` and publishes its box (`setTmuxPaneArea`, kept in `tmuxWindowSize.svelte.ts` as a box and never a size); the send effect keeps the box the last ask was for (`connection:widthxheight`, rounded) and asks only while the current box differs from it, or nothing was asked for this connection yet, and no drag holds it. While the box has moved, the effect tracks the fold and re-arms the 150 ms wait at each fold change, so the hosts' re-measure after a resize settles first; the wait reads the fold as it stands, sends it, and records the box. A fold change inside an unchanged box (the layout tmux answers with, a drag's preview, the hosts settling after tmux applied the last ask) runs nothing and asks nothing: that is what ends the fight. Dividers and ratios never produce a request.
- The drag hold. `Workspace.svelte` calls `startTmuxDrag` at the press and `endTmuxDrag(awaitingLayout)` at the release; the send effect asks nothing while held. A release that sent a request holds until the layout answering it (`setTmuxLayout`) or `TMUX_DRAG_ANSWER_MS` (one second), because the server submits `resize-pane` with no follow-up query (`Purpose::Fire`), so a request tmux refuses, or a layout lost with its connection, answers with nothing and must not hold the window size for the life of the window; a release that sent nothing, or whose request failed, frees it at once. A layout arriving during the press is not the answer and does not free it. With the fold as the value, the hold guards a box change that lands during a drag.
- The module's area setter reads what it keeps untracked: it runs inside the effect that observes, and an effect that depended on the state it writes would run itself again at each write (its cleanup wrote null, its body wrote the box, and Svelte stopped it at the update depth; the mounted test found it).
- Docs in the same change: the tmux paragraph of `web/packages/workspace-app/src/design.md`, the module doc of `tmuxWindowSize.svelte.ts`, and the comment over the effects in `App.svelte`. `crates/chan-library/design.md` says the request names "the grid the chan window can show", which still describes it.

## How it was read

Tests first, against `c43b29433`. `App.tmuxWindowSize.test.ts` mounts the app over the demo transport with two tmux pane tabs whose hosts the xterm fixture sizes at 60 by 40 cells (the fold is 121 by 40) and a 1210 by 800 px `main` box reported by a recording `ResizeObserver`; the demo PTY stand-in is wrapped so a pane session's `session` frame carries `tmux_pane`, as the server's does. Red on the base, for the right reasons: the drag's `expected [ [ 'pane-0', { ...(4) } ], ...(3) ] to deeply equal [ [ 'pane-0', { ...(4) } ] ]` (the three extra calls were `window-size` 141, 169 and 167 by 40 as the hosts re-measured around the `resize-pane`), the adopted layout's `expected [ [ 'pane-0', { ...(4) } ] ] to deeply equal []` (161 by 40), and the two hold pins' `expected [ [ 'pane-0', { ...(4) } ] ] to deeply equal []` (a `window-size` sent during the press, and one before tmux's layout answered the release). The resize pin, one `window-size` carrying the fold the hosts settle to (91 by 20 for a 1005 by 509 box, no quotient of the box), passes on the base, which asks on every fold change; it tells the fold apart from a size taken from the box, which would be 100 by 25. The unit file keeps the base's five `foldWindowGrid` pins and adds the box's and the hold's, which failed on the base on their missing exports (`setTmuxPaneArea is not a function`, `startTmuxDrag is not a function`). Green after the change: the two files with `Workspace.test.ts`, `App.paneMode.test.ts`, `TerminalTab.tmuxPane.test.ts`, `cloneTabFields.test.ts`, `terminalMoveAcrossWindows.test.ts`, `tmuxResize.test.ts`, `tmuxLayout.test.ts` and `store.tmuxTabs.test.ts`: 10 files, 123 tests; svelte-check 0 errors.

The real-tmux reading, on this machine at the tip: `target/debug/chan` built from the branch (sha256 `ae1c1844f98f3893e11f0a837033b57fd70cf8b889872c3a9fac552ffe7c9811`), tmux 3.6a, Chrome for Testing 126 headed at a 1400 by 900 viewport, ghostty panes at device pixel ratio 1, every chan process under `env -i` with a private `CHAN_HOME` and `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`. A private server, `tmux -L chan-fix new-session -d -s fix -x 120 -y 40` split once horizontally and once vertically (`%0 60x40, %1 59x20, %2 59x19`, window `120x40`), was attached through `tmux -L chan-fix -CC attach-session -t fix` typed into a local terminal opened from the launcher of `chan devserver run --port 0 --no-tunnel` with `terminal.tmux.enabled` set; the tmux window chan minted was opened from the launcher, and every `POST /api/terminals/<session>/tmux-request` of its page was logged with its body. Each drag pressed a `.divider`, moved in twelve steps and released, then waited 1.5 s before two reads one second apart with `tmux -L chan-fix list-panes -t fix -F '#{pane_id} #{pane_width}x#{pane_height}'` and `tmux -L chan-fix display-message -p -t fix '#{window_width}x#{window_height}'`. - Drag 3, the vertical divider at x 821 by -160 px: 10:35:08.347 `{"request":"resize-pane","pane":"%0","axis":"width","cells":79}`. Read 1 and read 2: `%0 79x44, %1 88x18, %2 88x25`, window `168x44`.

One `resize-pane` per drag and nothing else, no `window-size` after the attach's own, the window grid 168 by 44 across the three drags, and each pane at the size the drag asked for, resting across both reads.

The same reading against the base bundle (a `web/dist` built from the `web/` tree of `c43b29433`, served by the same debug binary over the same box) showed what the change removes: the attach asked for the same 168 by 44, and each drag's `resize-pane` was followed by `window-size` requests as the hosts re-measured, 169 by 44 then 168 by 44 after drag 1, 168 by 45, 168 by 46 and 168 by 44 after drag 2, 167 by 44, 168 by 44, 169 by 44 and 168 by 44 after drag 3, ten in all against the one above; in that run the correction damped within the 1.5 s wait (drag 2's pane asked for 18 rows and rested at 19), where the owner saw it sustained. A first cut that sized the window to the area's cells instead of the fold asked for 175 by 47 at the same box, three columns and a row past every host, which is why the value stays the fold.
