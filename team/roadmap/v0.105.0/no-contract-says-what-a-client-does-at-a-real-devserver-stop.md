# No contract says what a client does at a real devserver stop

Status: raised for v0.105.0 by the owner's word of 2026-10-09, from the v0.103.0 release report's follow-ups, which the v0.104.0 report does not repeat; written down, not designed, not accepted for build.

## What was seen

This is a left-over of the v0.103.0 report and not of the v0.104.0 report, which does not name it. `team/release/release-v0.103.0.md` lists under Follow-ups "the real-stop item's code-specific client contract or the partial measurement accepted" and says that whoever reopens the real-stop responses "starts from a new item, with the closed item's record of what was observed"; under What shipped, "Partial", it says no coded `devserver_stopping` 503 was observed by a client, "and whether to specify a code-specific client contract is the owner's decision". The v0.104.0 scope left it out as a stated cost: "a code-specific client contract is an owner decision nobody has asked for yet" (`dev/v0.104.0-scope.md` in the development tree, "Stated costs, not in scope"), and the closed host-holds item names "a real stop's client contract (a stated cost)" as not its own (`team/roadmap/done/three-holds-keep-the-workspace-host-alive.md`, Boundaries).

The closed item is the record (`team/roadmap/done/no-client-reads-the-devserver-stopping-code.md`, withdrawn at v0.103.0 with its measurement partial). What the server answers, from its "What was seen": from the stop signal on, a devserver's gate answers a tenant's request with 503, the sentence `the devserver is stopping`, the code `devserver_stopping` and no `Retry-After`, where a devserver that starts answers 503 with `Retry-After: 1`, so that a client can wait out a start and give up on a stop without reading words. What clients do with it, read in the v0.101 round at its builder's lane tip and not run: the shared wait of a browser window for its page retries every 503 each second for sixty seconds, the desktop's probe reads the status alone, and the workspace app's callers of `isTransientApiError` take the stop as transient.

What a real stop showed, in four arms (the closed item, "Browser measurement, 2026-10-06" and "Native connecting-page measurement, 2026-10-06"). Headless Chromium 154, the workspace app: the devserver exited before the next request, which failed with `net::ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED`, and the row showed `Failed to fetch`; one request, one transport failure, zero HTTP responses. Chromium, the launcher, the stop 33 ms after the click: the child exited six milliseconds after the signal, the one tenant page request had begun before it, and no tenant request after the stop was observed. Chromium, the launcher, the stop one millisecond before the click: the request began after the exit and failed with `net::ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED`, and the launcher showed `Failed to fetch`. Linux WebKitGTK, the desktop, the devserver killed: the connecting page showed `attempt 1: could not connect`, and recovery after the restart was not observed on the selected page. In none of the four did a client receive the coded 503, and none shows a request inside the stopping gate's lifetime. Three earlier launcher attempts failed on their instrument (a healthy request that was missing, an arm that never clicked, a target that changed) and give no verdict.

At the released tree, `af2af8ac0`, the shape stands, by a reading for this item that ran nothing. The gate's stopping arm answers the code and its starting arm the header (`crates/chan-server/src/devserver.rs` 3900 to 3916). `web/packages/web-shared/src/window-page.ts` throws on a refusal that is not a 503 and retries a 503 after its `Retry-After`, one second when the header is absent, inside a sixty-second deadline (9, 16, 81 to 87 and 181). `isTransientApiError` counts every 503 as transient (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/api/errors.ts` 81 to 88). The desktop's `probe_url` hands only the response's status to `probe_response_reachable`, for which a 503 is not reachable (`desktop/src-tauri/src/main.rs` 3785 to 3801 and 3850 to 3902). A search of the tree for the literal `devserver_stopping` outside `team/` and the changelog finds the server's two lines, two lines of the server's design document and two lines of one web test, which pins that an upload refused with 503 keeps the server's sentence and code (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/transfers.queue.test.ts` 243 and 251); no product file under `web/`, `desktop/`, `crates/chan` or `crates/chan-shell` names it.

Not established: that the code reaches any client at a real stop, and how often. The closed item reads the framework's source as saying that most clients of a stopping devserver meet a closed or refused connection, and the four arms agree with that without measuring a rate. What each client should show at a stop is in no record. Nothing was observed on macOS or Windows, through a gateway, or with a stop slow enough for a request to be dispatched inside its gate.

## Desired contract

This item asks for a decision, the owner's, between the two choices the v0.103.0 report names. One: specify a client contract for the code, so that a client that receives a stopping devserver's refusal tells it from a starting devserver's by its code and does not wait it out as it waits out a start. The other: accept the partial measurement, keep the clients as they are and record the unread code as a cost. The owner's condition of 2026-10-06 stands for either: the observations are used to propose client behaviour, and no rule for every 503 is inferred from this code.

## What to do

Put the decision to the owner with the four arms and their denominators. If the owner wants the missing observation first, its first step is an arm in which a request is dispatched inside the stopping gate, which no arm reached, the two stops that were timed having ended five and six milliseconds after their signal: a seat designs how a disposable devserver's stop is held open long enough, preflights the instrument on a healthy request, and records for each client what it receives and what it shows. A client change is designed after the answer, from what each client showed.

## Boundaries

By the closed item's citations: `web/packages/web-shared/src/window-page.ts`, the desktop's probe and its connecting page, and the workspace app's callers of `isTransientApiError`, with their tests; fixtures under `scripts/e2e/` for a measurement. Not changed: what the devserver answers (the status, the sentence, the code, no `Retry-After`) and the refusal envelope; the window list's and the window feed's refusals, which carry neither the code nor the header by design (`crates/chan-server/design.md`, "Window snapshots while a devserver starts or stops"); a starting devserver's 503, which a client still waits out; a 503 from a proxy or from the transfer bound, which is not a stop.

## Acceptance

2. If the measurement is completed first: at least one request is shown dispatched inside the stopping gate, with the status, the body's code and the headers it received and what the client showed, for the browser's shared wait, a workspace API caller and the desktop's probe, the engine named for each; an arm that cannot be produced is recorded as not produced, with its attempts.
3. If a contract is built: each client that reads a devserver's 503 has a pin of what it does with the code `devserver_stopping`, red first, and a pin that a starting devserver's 503 with `Retry-After` is still waited out.
4. If a contract is built: `make web-check` and the desktop crate's tests are green at the commits in the owning guests, and the server's answer at a stop is unchanged, shown by its existing test.
