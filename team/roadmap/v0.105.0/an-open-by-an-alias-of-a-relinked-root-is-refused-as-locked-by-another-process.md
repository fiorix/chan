# An open by an alias of a relinked root is refused as locked by another process

Status: raised for v0.105.0 by the owner's word of 2026-10-09, from the v0.103.0 release report's follow-ups, which the v0.104.0 report does not repeat; written down, not designed, not accepted for build.

## What was seen

This is a left-over of the v0.103.0 report and not of the v0.104.0 report, which does not name it. `team/release/release-v0.103.0.md` says under What shipped that a relinked mounted workspace is handed off through its registered row, with the "Recorded limit: an open by an alias of a relinked root answers `WorkspaceLocked` with a reason that misleads, outside the accepted contract", and lists under Follow-ups "the relinked-handoff item's alias-open refusal reason". The v0.104.0 scope left it out as a stated cost (`dev/v0.104.0-scope.md` in the development tree, "Stated costs, not in scope").

The record is the closed item's "Recorded limit, 2026-10-06" (`team/roadmap/done/a-root-relinked-while-mounted-cannot-be-handed-off.md`). An open by an alias spelling of a root that was relinked since it was mounted is answered that the workspace is locked, for a workspace this process holds. The registered open finds a mounted row only by the root the registry stores, which is the resolved path, and the launcher's add route registers first and then passes the request's own spelling, not its row's root, to the open. The item calls it "a refusal with a misleading reason, reached only by a relink under a mounted workspace plus an open by another spelling", outside its accepted contract, with nothing changed for it. Three `chan-library` relink tests met the answer in the gate's symlinked-temp arm, where they asked the registered open by a temporary path's alias; they were repaired to ask by the stored root (the same section; the v0.103.0 report, What shipped, "Gate and browser suite").

At the released tree, `af2af8ac0`, by a reading for this item that ran nothing. The idempotent open selects a registry row only where the row's stored root equals the given spelling after lexical normalization (`open_or_get_registered_workspace_used`, `crates/chan-library/src/host.rs` 2383 to 2390), and its doc says "Callers of a new spelling must resolve it to the stored row before entering here" (2310 to 2313); with no row selected it looks for a runtime by the key the spelling resolves to now, and otherwise opens anew (2474 to 2484). The launcher's add registers the root, derives the prefix from the row's stored root and opens with the request's own path (`add_workspace`, `crates/chan-server/src/routes/library.rs` 2263, 2274 and 2279), where the row's on opens with the stored root (2337 to 2341). The add answers a `WorkspaceLocked` with 409 and "This workspace is open in another chan process. Quit it and try again." (`workspace_open_elsewhere`, `crates/chan-server/src/error.rs` 129 to 138); the error's own text is "workspace is locked by another process" (`crates/chan-workspace/src/error.rs` 41 to 42). Why a second open of a relinked mounted root reads this process's own writer lock as another's is in the closed item's older "What was seen": the lock's record names the path the workspace was mounted at, and the open compares it with the path the root resolves to now.

Not established: the refusal in a run through the launcher's add or any other product route; the three reds were library tests that asked by a temporary path's alias, and the route's part is a reading. Whether passing the row's root to the open would serve the case, and what spelling the desktop's own call passes: the closed item says neither was traced. How a user comes to relink a mounted root and then open it by another spelling, and how often. The three reds are recorded from the v0.103 round's Linux gate, where the Windows target was linted and not run, and the reproduction from two tests of that round, on a platform the closed item does not name; whether the relink tests run in the macOS and Windows CI jobs was not read for this item.

## Desired contract

An open of a workspace that this process holds, asked by an alias of a root relinked since its mount, does not answer that another chan process holds it. Either it reaches the mounted runtime through its registered row, as the handoff and the row's on do since v0.103.0, or it is refused with a reason that is true. The record does not choose between the two; the trace below does. The writer lock is not weakened to suppress the answer, by the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 on the parent item.

## What to do

Reproduce first, through the route: a test that mounts a workspace, relinks its root as the parent item's reproduction did (the parent directory moved and replaced by a symlink) and posts the launcher's add with an alias spelling, reading the status and the sentence. Then trace the two things the closed item left untraced: what the open answers when the add passes its row's stored root instead of the request's spelling, and what spelling the desktop's own open passes. Then a short design to the reviewer that names which of the two contracts each caller gets, before code.

## Boundaries

`crates/chan-server/src/routes/library.rs` (the launcher's add and the errors it maps), `crates/chan-library/src/host.rs` (the idempotent open's row selection), the desktop's call under `desktop/src-tauri/src/` if the trace shows that it passes an alias, their tests, and the design sentences that state the open's contract. Not changed: the writer lock and its comparison; the handoff and the row's on as repaired in v0.103.0, with their pins and the four mutations that guard them; the rule that an unmounted row whose path redirects to another registered row cannot borrow that row's runtime; a direct host call by an arbitrary new spelling with no registration, which the parent item places outside its contract.
