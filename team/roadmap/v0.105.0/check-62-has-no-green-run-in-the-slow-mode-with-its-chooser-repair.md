# Check 62 has no green run in the slow mode with its chooser repair

Status: raised for v0.105.0 by the owner's word of 2026-10-09, from the v0.103.0 release report's follow-ups, which the v0.104.0 report does not repeat; written down, not designed, not accepted for build.

## What was seen

v0.103.0 repaired a race in browser check 62 with one in-page call before the chooser command of its 256-file probe (`scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/checks/62-binary-transfer-streaming.mjs`, line 875 at the released tree), and recorded what the repair did not show. [check-62-asks-for-a-file-chooser-without-a-user-gesture](../done/check-62-asks-for-a-file-chooser-without-a-user-gesture.md), Acceptance 2: met in the fast mode, the committed check green alone at 0.611 cores with no software WebGL warning, and "A green slow-mode run is not shown". The v0.103.0 report's Follow-ups (`team/release/release-v0.103.0.md`): "The check 62 repair is shown green alone in the fast mode only; a slow-mode green with the repair is not shown, and the check's thirty-second probe wait is a second load-sensitive wait of the check." The slow mode, in that item's Finding: the guest's two cores at their quota (1.99 and 2.00 cores at 59% and 70% CPU pressure) with Chrome's software WebGL fallback line in the page. In its constructed pair, both arms paused six seconds before the chooser command, the arm with the repair passed the chooser and then failed the probe's thirty-second wait, `coalescing probe upload did not finish` (lines 903 and 904 at the released tree); the item's Reading says of that wait that "neither its root layer nor a product stall is proved".

v0.104.0 met the check red again under load, three times at the same wait, in runs of 2026-10-08 recorded on two closed items. On [the-welcome-runs-webgl-on-a-software-context](../done/the-welcome-runs-webgl-on-a-software-context.md), "Measurement, 2026-10-08": check 62 alone and unchanged, twice with the welcome forced to a fullscreen fragment animation and twice with it off; both runs with it on failed on the check's own assertions (the process-memory growth before any chooser, and the 30 s upload deadline after choosers of 0.9 to 1.5 s), and both runs with it off passed in about 108 s. On [a-command-triggered-upload-with-no-gesture-opens-no-chooser](../done/a-command-triggered-upload-with-no-gesture-opens-no-chooser.md), "Landed 2026-10-08", with `dev/v0104-team/reports/validation-Frontend104-item5.md`, "Failed attempts and corrections retained" and "Four-run check 62 comparison": the stock run `browser-62` failed at the unchanged thirty-second coalescing deadline with no animation id or renderer recorded, and of four instrumented cells the default-motion cell at the tip failed the same deadline under a WebGL2 welcome (`fourteenfold-bloom`, 197.78 percent of one core under a 200 percent quota, the 256-file upload at 21 percent in its failure screenshot), while the two reduced-motion cells (43.73 and 35.66 percent of one core) and the default-motion cell at the base on a 2D animation (58.57 percent) passed with the probe done in 6.4 to 8.4 s. Each cell is one run, and the two default-motion cells mounted different animations.

The greens with the repair since then, none of them in the slow mode. With the welcome's 2D fallback landed, check 62 passed alone once with a 2D welcome on SwiftShader at 58.51 percent of one core (the upload item's "Acceptance 6 closed 2026-10-08"). It passed in the whole run and alone in both browser matrices of the release; its alone leg took 107.4 s at 0.619 cores with 2 throttled periods at the candidate and 107.5 s at 0.741 cores with 2 throttled periods at the GA commit, in a guest capped at 4 CPUs and 8 GiB (`dev/v0104-team/evidence/Desktop104/browser/candidate-matrix-01.summary.json` and `ga-matrix-01.summary.json`, the leg `alone-62-binary-transfer-streaming`, read for this item with nothing run).

Engine, for the v0.104.0 runs: Chrome for Testing 155, headless, on SwiftShader in Linux guests with no GPU, the candidate's matrix on debug binaries; the v0.103.0 runs were headless Chrome in a guest of two CPUs with no GPU, and their Chrome version was not read for this item. The v0.104.0 measurement says the check makes no trusted click, so its chooser waits are intercepted headless chooser events after scripted activation.

Not established: a green run of the repaired check with the guest's CPU at its quota; every run that the records above place at its quota is red, and every green above whose CPU is stated ran at about a third to three quarters of one core. What waits in the probe's thirty seconds when it fails: the page starved by the animation, the transfer itself, or the check's own poll. Whether the slow mode still arises on the released tree, where the welcome draws 2D on a renderer that names itself as software, as Chrome's does in the guest, and keeps the whole catalog on a masked renderer. Whether `browser-62` ran under a WebGL2 welcome: the upload item's record counts it so, and the validation report says no animation id or renderer was recorded for it. Whether a user's upload of many files is slowed the same way by a busy page: no report from use. Nothing here was observed in a native webview or on a hardware GPU.

## Desired contract

Check 62 with its chooser repair is shown under the slow mode: green with the guest's CPU at its quota, or red with a record that says what waited in the probe's thirty seconds. If the slow mode can no longer be made on the released tree without changing the check, the item asks for a decision: record the slow mode as retired by the welcome's fallback and the wait as a stated load-sensitive bound, or change the bound, which is a change of criterion and needs its own explicit decision, as the owner ruled on 2026-10-06 for the suite.

## What to do

Read first whether the slow mode can still be made without touching the check: a welcome forced to a WebGL2 animation, as the v0.104.0 measurement did, or a narrower CPU quota on the run's unit. Run check 62 alone under it several times with the suite's failure record and the unit's CPU, throttle and pressure counters, naming the mounted welcome and the renderer in each run. For a red, say from the record where the thirty seconds went. Report before any change to the check or the product.

## Boundaries

`scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/checks/62-binary-transfer-streaming.mjs`, the suite's runner and failure record under `scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/`, and whatever starts the run's unit. Not changed without an explicit decision: the check's assertions and bounds. Not changed at all by this item: the product's upload, streaming and coalescing paths, the chooser repair, and the upload request that v0.104.0 shipped. A product wait that the record places gets an item of its own.

## Acceptance

1. A record of how the load was made and of each run under it: the unit's quota, the cores used, the throttled periods and the CPU pressure, the mounted welcome and the renderer; a run that is not at its quota is not counted as a slow-mode run.
2. At least one run of the repaired check at its quota that is green, or a red whose record says what waited in the probe's thirty seconds; every red is kept beside the greens.
3. No run among them shows the activation warning or a chooser timeout, or the run that shows one is reported as a red of the repair itself.
4. If the slow mode cannot be made: the decision is recorded here, and the release report says the slow mode is unshown and why.
5. If a bound of the check is changed: by an explicit decision, with the measurement that sets the new bound, and with the earlier reds kept in the record.
