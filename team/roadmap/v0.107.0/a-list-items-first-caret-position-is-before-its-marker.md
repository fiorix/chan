# A list item's first caret position is before its marker

Status: built on 2026-10-16 on the seat's branch; the section Build 2026-10-16 holds the evidence; it closes at the GA as shipped.

## What was seen

Since the list prefix repair (`2bc8a2f38`) an item's indent, marker and gap are one atomic span, so ArrowLeft from the start of the item's text lands at the line start, before the glyph, in one press, and the caret can rest there. What is typed there goes into the source in front of the marker: a Space indents the item (the owner's own sequence of ArrowLeft, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Space and a word now yields ` * hellotwo`, rendered as before with the source line carrying a leading space), and a letter turns the item into a paragraph (`h* two`, the raw marker shown). A click left of the glyph lands at the nearer edge of the prefix, which is the line start. The repair's row records these under "What changes" and "Open"; that row's caret filter (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/decorations/list_prefix.ts`) keeps the caret off the inside of the prefix and treats both edges as resting places.

## Owner's decision 2026-10-15

Put to the owner by survey on 2026-10-15 ("Editor: can the caret rest before a list item's glyph?") with two options, the Google Docs model recommended. The owner's answer, in the survey's words: "Google Docs model: the text start is the item's first position (recommended, follow-up row)". Applied as this row.

## Desired contract

The start of an item's text is the item's first caret position. ArrowLeft from it leaves the line for the end of the line above, and on the document's first line it stays; Home goes to the text start; a click or a drag that starts left of the glyph lands at the text start; a selection reaching the item from above ends at the text start or takes the whole line, never a part of the prefix; nothing can be typed in front of a marker. The source keeps whatever indent it has: no whitespace is rewritten. The atomic prefix, Enter, Tab and Shift+Tab stay as they are; Backspace at the text start is [backspace-at-an-items-text-start-removes-its-indent-with-the-marker](backspace-at-an-items-text-start-removes-its-indent-with-the-marker.md).

## What to do

Design first, in the caret filter: the line start of a parsed item becomes a position the caret passes through and does not rest on, with the direction read as the filter reads it now (a caret arriving from the text goes on to the end of the line above; one arriving from above or from a click goes to the text start). List every path that can land there and what each does: ArrowLeft and ArrowRight, Home and End, Shift with each, a click and a drag on and left of the glyph, Ctrl+Home and a selection of the whole document, ArrowUp and ArrowDown from a longer line above or below, a restored selection, a search match at the line start, and the first line of the document where there is no line above. Name which of those keep the line start as a selection anchor (selecting whole lines must still work) and which move. Then the repair, with a state-level pin per path red first, and the owner's sequence re-read in the browser with the repair's driver (`* one` then `* two`: the two ArrowLefts now leave the item for the one above, and the word lands at its end, `* one hello`).

## Boundaries

`web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/decorations/list_prefix.ts` and `blocks.listPrefix.test.ts`, `editor/design.md`; `editor/commands/list.ts` only if a command places the caret at the line start. Not changed: the atomic prefix and the walker; the document's text; Enter, Tab and Shift+Tab; Backspace, which has its own row.

## Acceptance

1. ArrowLeft from the text start of an item below another line lands at the end of the line above; on the document's first line it stays at the text start; pinned red first.
2. Home from inside an item's text lands at the text start, and Shift+Home selects back to the text start, pinned.
3. A click or a drag start left of the glyph lands at the text start, pinned at the state level and read once in the browser.
4. Every path the design lists is pinned with what it does, and no path leaves an empty selection at an item's line start.
5. The owner's sequence in the browser, with the repair's driver, types the word in order at the end of the item above.
6. The existing editor suites, svelte-check and the whole workspace-app suite green; `make web-check` green in the owning guest.

## Lead's rulings 2026-10-16

The frontend seat's design put three points to the lead, who ruled on 2026-10-16, in its words:

1. "The task box is inside the pass-through region: the caret never rests beside or inside a task's box, the item's text start is its first caret position for a task as for a bullet, and the box stays clickable through its own widget. No keyboard route toggles a box today (`widgets/checkbox.ts` toggles only from its own mousedown and cancels Space on a focused box; `commands/format.ts` `toggleTaskList` changes the prefix, not the box), so nothing reachable is lost. The base pin "the task checkbox stays reachable beside its atomic neighbours" is rewritten to the new contract: the box toggles on a click and is passed by the caret."
2. "A drag that starts left of the glyph and leaves the line keeps the line start as its anchor, so the whole first line, marker included, is selected and copied; a drag that stays on the line anchors at the text start; a triple-click's whole-line range keeps the line start. That is the contract's "takes the whole line, never a part of the prefix"."

Ruling 2 narrowed on 2026-10-16 after the review's read of the range, in the lead's words: a drag that starts left of the glyph and leaves the line downward or past the line's end keeps the line start as its anchor, so the whole first line is selected; a drag that leaves the line upward ends at the text start, since a selection reaching an item from above ends at the text start or takes the whole line, by the owner's contract; a drag that stays on the line anchors at the text start; a triple-click's whole-line range keeps the line start.
3. "The Home binding lives in `listPrefixCaret()` inside `list_prefix.ts`, within the item's boundary, and reaches every editor that installs `chanDecorations()`; it returns false off an item line and on a wrapped item's later visual line so the default runs. Accepted."

## Build 2026-10-16

Built in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/decorations/list_prefix.ts`, the design read first by the lead and the review seat (`reports/design-Frontend107-lstfst.md` with its "Revisions 2026-10-16", and `reviews/review-Review107-2-lstfst-design.md`, in the round's bus). Each parsed item line has a region from its line start to its text start, `itemRegion`, the task box and the gap after it included (ruling 1), read through `parsedListItemAt`: the prefix regex confirmed by a `ListMark` in the syntax tree, so a list-shaped line that is not an item keeps every position. The transaction filter of `listPrefixCaret()` keeps every caret out of a region. A keyboard step (userEvent `select`, not a pointer's) whose head leaves the text start goes to the end of the line above, and stays at the text start on the document's first line; any other empty caret there (a click, a blank-area click, a vertical landing, a restore or a find match, an undo, a typed change) goes to the text start, with its goal column, association and bidi level. A range end in a region goes to the line start when the range takes the line whole (its other end past the line's end, or at the end of the document's last line) and to the text start otherwise, except that a Shift step left from the text start reaches the end of the line above (ruling 2). Home and mac Cmd-ArrowLeft and Ctrl-a, with their Shift forms, are bound in a `Prec.high` keymap that `listPrefixCaret()` returns beside the filter (ruling 3), because from a text start their transaction is ArrowLeft's: on an item line whose line-start target is at or before the text start they put the head at the text start, and they return false off an item, on a selection of several ranges, and for Home on a wrapped item's later visual line, so the default runs there. The atomic prefix and the walker, the document's text, Enter, Tab and Shift-Tab are unchanged; `editor/design.md` "Atomic widgets" describes the contract.

What a reader meets: the owner's sequence (`* one`, `* two`, the caret at `two`, ArrowLeft, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Space, `hello`) types ` hello` at the end of `* one`; nothing can be typed in front of a marker; a task's box toggles on a click and the caret passes it. Since the line start is no longer a caret position, Enter at an item's first caret position leaves an empty item above it, which is `continueListOnEnter`'s rule for a caret right after the marker; the mounted keymap case that set the caret at the line start of `- hello` and expected Enter to open a line above it (`editor/wysiwygListSplitKeymap.test.ts`) is rewritten to that.

The pins are `editor/decorations/blocks.listPrefix.test.ts`, rewritten to the contract: 92 cases, the paths of the design and the review's additions on `*`, `-`, ordered, nested and task items, driven through the editor's keymaps with real `keydown` events (Ctrl as the word step and Alt as the syntax step on jsdom), the mac line-start commands run as the keymap runs them, vertical landings dispatched as the `select` transactions they are (jsdom lays out nothing for `moveVertically`), and pointer selections dispatched with `select.pointer` and `select.pointer.blank`. The sweep runs over a document of every shape (a first-line item, an indented item, `- [ ]` and `- [x]` tasks, a `10.` item, an empty `* ` item, a list-shaped line that is not an item, a last line with no trailing newline), with the regions written out from the fixture's own prefix lengths and checked against the parser: a caret set at every position by a restore and by a click, every arrow, word, syntax, Home and End key with and without Shift and the mac line-start commands from every position, every range between two positions, and a typed character, Backspace, Enter, Tab and Shift-Tab at every position with undo after each; no empty selection rests in a region and no range end sits in one except a line start the range takes whole.

## Lead's ruling 2026-10-16, the parse's progress

`make web-check` at the landed tip in the integration guest (four CPUs under a throttled quota, more vitest workers) failed the sweep "a caret set at every position" with 28 breaches: carets resting in the region of every item from the fifth line on (`evidence/Lead107/int/gate-lstfst-landed/` in the round's bus). The same bytes were green in the frontend guest.

The trigger, read in the installed `@codemirror/language` 6.12.4: a state's first parse works for 20 ms up to the first 3000 characters and keeps the tree it has when the time runs out; a transaction that changes no text keeps the language state, so a selection never moves the parse on; the view parses the rest on an idle callback, which never turns inside a synchronous sweep. The filter confirms an item through `syntaxTree(state)`, so under load a sweep's later items were no items for the whole sweep. In the product the same tree means a line the parser has not reached shows its marker as text, and the filter leaves a caret there; the parse that reaches the line arrived as a transaction with no selection and no change, which the filter passed, and the caret then sat inside the hidden prefix until the next selection change, against the contract.

The lead's ruling, 2026-10-16, in its words: "the filter also places the selection on a transaction whose tree differs from its start state's (`syntaxTree(tr.state)` not `syntaxTree(tr.startState)`, one reference compare), by the same rule (a caret in a region to its text start; a range end by `endFor`), so the filter and the decorations keep reading one tree and the caret follows the decorations when the parse arrives; no parse is forced inside the filter", with "the sweep file parses its fixture whole (`forceParsing` to the document's end, asserted) before any sweep, so the sweeps test the filter and not the worker's speed".

Built: the filter in `list_prefix.ts` runs on a transaction that changes the tree as on one that sets a selection or changes the document. The suite's `mount` parses its fixture whole (`ensureSyntaxTree` to the document's end, refused past 10 s) and takes the finished tree in with the filters off, so a test's starting caret stays where the test puts it. A new case, "a caret on a line the parse had not reached goes to the text start when the parse reaches it", builds its state under a clock that runs the first parse's budget out after one step, places a caret on the last item's line start, and parses. The editor design text says the filter follows the parse.
