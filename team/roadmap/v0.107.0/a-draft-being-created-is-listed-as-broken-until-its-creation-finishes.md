# A draft being created is listed as broken until its creation finishes

Status: built on 2026-10-16 on the seat's branch; the section A draft is listed whole or not at all 2026-10-16 holds the evidence; it closes at the GA as shipped.

Record before the release: raised by the review seat on 2026-10-13 from the forty recorded timelines of check 131's loop (N2 of `reviews/review-Review106-chk131-recorder-1.md` in the round's bus); written down the same day by the lead as a row of its own, outside that item; owner the frontend seat with the server; not reached in the v0.106.0 round

## What was seen

In the forty recorded runs of check 131 (`evidence/Frontend106/chk131-loop-{idle,churn}/` in the round's bus), 128 of 288 `GET /api/drafts` polls answered 200 with a `broken_draft` warning for a draft whose creation was in flight: 107 read "draft identity is unavailable: No such file or directory (os error 2)" and 21 "draft has no primary file", each with a source the page can offer to discard (`store.svelte.ts` 630 at the round's base). The warned polls sit between a `POST /api/drafts/new` request and its response: the 80 creations took a median of 41 ms idle and 46 ms under churn, 21 ms at the shortest, 1.2 s idle and 6.3 s under churn at the longest, four over a second; the longest run of consecutive warned polls was 5.8 s. So a list read during a creation reports the draft as damaged when it is unfinished. Not read: when the page refreshes its list and whether a person sees the warning or a discard offer during a slow creation; whether the warning can outlive the creation.

## Desired contract

A draft whose creation is in flight is either absent from the list or listed as being created; it is never listed as broken, and the page never offers to discard it, until its creation has finished or failed.

## What to do

Read the create route and the list route for the window between the lifetime's registration and its primary file's write, and the page's list refresh (how often, and what it shows for a `broken_draft` row); construct the window in a route test (the create held between the two writes while the list is read) and read the page's behaviour once in a browser with a slowed creation. Then decide: the list skips lifetimes whose creation is in flight, or the create registers the lifetime only after its files exist, or the page holds a broken row for a grace period before offering to discard. Design before code; the review seat reads the design.

Changed by the lead's rulings of 2026-10-16 on the runtime seat's design note (`reports/design-Runtime107-dbroke.md`) and the review seat's read of it (`reviews/review-Review107-68-dbroke-design.md`, both in the round's bus). The create registers the lifetime only after its files exist: the draft is built in a staging sibling of `Drafts/`, marker then seeded primary, and published by one rename that refuses an existing name, so a draft in flight is absent from the list and from the workspace warnings, and a creation that fails leaves nothing behind. The runtime seat's reading found the same fault at the promotion, which removes its source with `remove_dir_all` while the source is still under `Drafts/`, where the list's preflight can read it as broken; the promotion retires its source into staging by one rename before removing it, with its own pin and mutant. A retired copy the promotion then cannot remove answers success with one warning, a change from the base's `DraftBroken`: the target holds the draft and `Drafts/` no longer does, so the state the base's error named no longer exists, and the copy is disk only until a later open sweeps it. The page needs no change for an absent draft; its reading during a held creation is the frontend seat's on the landed tip, with the test seam's hold.

## Boundaries

`crates/chan-server/src/routes/drafts.rs` (create and list), the drafts store in `chan-workspace`, the page's drafts list (`state/drafts.svelte.ts`, `components/DraftsGroup.svelte`) and their tests. Not changed: what a truly broken draft shows and offers.

Changed by the lead's rulings of 2026-10-16: the drafts store, the `drafts` module's promotion, the sidecar paths, the forget list and the metadata archive's exclusions in `chan-workspace`, with a test seam behind its `test-hooks` feature; `crates/chan-server/src/routes/drafts.rs` (the create) and its tests. The page's drafts list is unchanged. The standalone create is unchanged too: its own store and reader list a directory, never a broken draft. The publish is atomic within one filesystem (`RENAME_NOREPLACE` on Linux, `RENAME_EXCL` on macOS) or one volume (`MoveFileExW` on Windows), which `Drafts/` and its staging sibling share unless one is a mount point. A mount answers `EXDEV` or `ERROR_NOT_SAME_DEVICE` and the create fails through its cleanup with nothing copied. A filesystem without the no-replace flag takes the check-then-rename fallback, where an outside writer's empty `Drafts/<name>` made between the two is replaced rather than refused; that is the fallback's cost. On Windows a scanner holding a just-written file refuses the move, as a discard's move into the draft trash already can.

## A draft is listed whole or not at all 2026-10-16

Built as the lead ruled, on the runtime seat's branch on top of `8c0eab57f`, which differs from `566d5bbad`, where the red ran, only in a test constant's `cfg(unix)`. `chan-workspace`'s `DraftStore::create_draft` builds a workspace draft in a private entry under `drafts-staging/` (the lifetime marker, then the seeded primary) and publishes it as `Drafts/<name>` by one `no_replace::rename` under the store's lock; a create that fails before the rename removes its entry. The name is reserved from the check to the publish through a guard that releases it on any return or unwind, and `next_untitled_name` skips a reserved name. `Workspace::create_draft` replaces the route's two steps for notes and diagrams and answers the ID the marker holds. A promotion retires its source into `drafts-staging/` by one rename before removing it there; a retired copy it cannot remove is logged at WARN and the promotion answers its target. A workspace open sweeps staging entries stamped more than an hour ago. Forget and unregister remove `drafts-staging/`, and the metadata archive lists it among its exclusions. The seam `chan_workspace::draft_hold`, compiled only for tests and `test-hooks` builds, holds or fails one step of a create or a promotion (`directory-made`, `marker-written`, `primary-written`, `source-removing`); a server built with `cargo build -p chan --features chan-workspace/test-hooks` and started with `CHAN_TEST_DRAFT_HOLD=<step>:<absolute dir>` holds the first operation at that step, writes `<step> <directory>` to the FIFO `<dir>/reached`, and goes on when a writer opens the FIFO `<dir>/release`, or fails the operation with a timed-out error after 600 s.
