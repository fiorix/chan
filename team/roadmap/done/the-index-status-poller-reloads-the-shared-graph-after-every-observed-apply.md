# The index-status poller reloads the shared graph after every observed apply

Status: shipped in [v0.109.0](../../release/release-v0.109.0.md).

Record before the release: built on 2026-10-22 on the seat's branch; the section Built 2026-10-22 holds the evidence; it closes at the GA as shipped.

## What was seen

The index-status poller in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/store.svelte.ts` polls every 250 ms while the indexer reports a per-file reindex, and `graphReloadWhenReady` is set because `graphIndexUnavailable` counts that state as the graph being unavailable; when the indexer returns to idle the poller calls `reloadGraph()`, whose `invalidateGraph` (`state/graphData.svelte.ts`) cancels a pending quiet window and fetches the whole graph at once. The graph route answers gated only during a full rebuild, so since `60e73ad0c` every return to idle after a poll that observed a per-file reindex ends in a redundant whole-graph fetch, and with the apply frames the graph-refresh item added it lands beside the two fetches that item's design costs and cancels a pending burst window. Browser check 163 (`scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/checks/163-file-browser-watch-refresh.mjs`) alone in the idle integration guest was red 11 of 15 at the integrated tip, 4 of 10 at the graph-refresh item's tip (and 1 of 3 under load), and 1 of 5 before its page half. The reds at the graph-refresh tip are a single fetch 103 to 162 ms after a step's frames, the poller's reload cancelling the step's window, and the others are steps with three or four fetches. One red at the graph-refresh tip is on another assertion (the unopened directory caused a root listing) and is placed apart. The check models neither the apply frames nor the reload.

## Desired contract

The poller reloads the shared graph only when the graph route was gated (a full build, a recovery or the gated mark); an observed per-file apply changes nothing in a page that holds an ungated view; check 163 takes the apply frames as part of a step.

## What to do

`graphReloadWhenReady` takes `building`, `recovering` and the gated mark, not `reindexing`, with three pins: a reindex that returns to idle with an ungated cached view fetches nothing; a build that returns to idle reloads; a gated view reloads at idle. In the same commit check 163 takes the apply frames into its steps, each step's boundary after the frames and the fetch they cue: by the graph-refresh item's design a step that stays open past its apply holds two fetches, so the step model is wrong without this adjustment whatever the poller's repair does to the counts. The commit is proved by check 163 alone ten times in the seat's guest and three times beside a compile load there, and at the landing in the integration guest, beside the base's count.

## Boundaries

`state/store.svelte.ts` at the poller, `state/graphData.svelte.ts` only if the pins need a seam, their tests, and check 163. Not changed: the graph routes, the indexer, the quiet window.

## Built 2026-10-22

The poller reloads the shared graph at idle only after a state in which the graph route can answer gated: `graphIndexUnavailable` in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/store.svelte.ts` takes `recovering` and `building`, and the poller keeps its gated-mark clause, so a per-file `reindexing` that returns to idle reloads nothing in a page holding an ungated view. Check 163 (`scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/checks/163-file-browser-watch-refresh.mjs`) takes the apply frames into its steps: a step ends once an apply frame names a path it changed and a fetch has started after its last frame of either kind; the setup's 81 files are such a step before the first, and the setup waits for the workspace's first index build (idle and ready, as check 112 waits), since a full build takes in files written while it runs without an apply frame; a step is judged by its quiet windows (its frames grouped by gaps of 250 ms), each costing at most one fetch, the last fetch at least 200 ms after the last frame, with the one-fetch race allowed in at most three steps. As the lead ruled in its 06:09Z followup, every socket frame is stamped on receipt by the clock the listings carry, and the unopened directory's step counts only the listings that start at or after its own first frame, failing with the frames' paths and stamps. The workspace app's design text (`src/design.md`, the shared graph through a burst) says when the poller reloads.
