## The slow-mode runs and the lead's ruling 2026-10-13

By the hygiene seat in its guest at the integration commit `9c82839fe`, no check or product change (`reports/chkslw-reading-Hygiene106.md` and `tasks/task-Hygiene106-Lead106-29.md` in the round's bus; `evidence/Hygiene106/chkslw-1/`). The slow mode can still be made without touching the check, though not on the product as shipped: a guest-only browser preload saves a WebGL2 welcome (`tenfold-dahlia`) and masks the renderer classification so that the welcome's software-renderer fallback lets it mount; the canvas itself drew on SwiftShader in every run (278 to 308 draw calls before the probe), read from the mounted canvas. Acceptance 1: check 62 alone five times on Chrome for Testing 155.0.8059.39, the unit at `cpu.max` 2 CPUs and 6 GiB in every bracket, 1.99 to 2.00 cores used across the probe's thirty seconds, 312 to 314 throttled periods, the unit's CPU pressure 69 to 74 percent, the host's full CPU pressure zero, no memory event: five runs at their quota. Acceptance 2: each red on the unchanged wait ("coalescing probe upload did not finish", thirty seconds), none green and none skipped; by about 29 s the workspace had committed 13 to 18 of the probe's 256 files and the page showed 5 to 7 percent; the check's own 58 to 71 progress polls returned, and their round trips took 25 to 26 of the thirty seconds (228 to 585 ms each). The thirty seconds went to slow page round trips while the transfer was still committing files, not to a chooser wait and not to a frozen poll. Acceptance 3: five chooser events per run, no activation warning, no missing user gesture and no chooser timeout in any log, beside the five failure lines as the search's positive control. The record does not separate the CPU spent drawing from the CPU spent on the upload.

The lead's ruling: kept as a stated cost, and nothing changed. The chooser repair shows no fault under the slow mode. The check's thirty-second wait is a load-sensitive bound when a WebGL2 welcome draws on a software renderer at a two-CPU quota, a state the shipped fallback does not mount; no green run exists in it, and none is claimed. The bound stays as it is (acceptance 5 does not apply), the slow mode was made (acceptance 4 does not apply), and no product wait is placed by the record, so no item is raised from it.
