# Coming forward to a minimized window may not activate the app

Status: shipped in [v0.129.0](../../release/release-v0.129.0.md) for the three cases of its first contract line. A second launch on a minimized window and on a window behind another app, and a close confirmation and a Window menu row on a minimized window, each passed 10 of 10 on the owner's Mac on 2026-11-13 (macOS 27.0.1, a build of `8a44e110e`), and nothing took focus unasked. A window of an app hidden with Cmd+H failed on that walk: a second launch opened a new terminal window in 4 of 10 runs instead of raising the one the app held (the installed v0.128.0 left Terminal in front in 6 of 10 and went from 1 window to 7). It was fixed the same day (`eccf95adb`, `949bbb06b`; the section A hidden app), proven on Linux by tests and cross-target type checks only; on macOS: the owner's check of it (batch 6: parts H1 to H4 and a two-window part, on `d52db8b6a`) had not come back at the release commit, and the tag waits for it; the release record has its result. The walk's base control on the installed v0.128.0 passed the four other cases 10 of 10 too, so on that Mac the gap this row was raised for did not show; the Linux reading under metacity in the section Built is where the first fix's change was seen.

Record before the release: built in v0.129.0 on 2026-11-12 ahead of the owner's Mac reading, on the owner's word the same day (the section at the end); the owner's Mac walk of the fix is pending. At the v0.128.0 cut on 2026-11-12 it was carried to v0.129.0 not started, with the owner's Mac reading of the raise to decide whether a fix is built.

Record before the v0.128.0 release: raised on 2026-11-10 by the review of v0.127.0's single-instance guard before its GA, from reading; not observed on a Mac. Accepted for v0.128.0 by the owner the same day. Not started.

## What is there now

When a second launch asks the running desktop to come forward, `come_forward` (`desktop/src-tauri/src/single_instance.rs`) raises the last focused window that still shows by calling `unminimize`, `show` and `set_focus` one after the other. On macOS the windowing library's `set_focus` does nothing while the window still reads as miniaturized (tao 0.37.2, `platform_impl/macos/window.rs`), and it is the only one of the three calls that activates the app. If the window has not finished leaving the Dock when the third call runs, the window is restored but chan stays behind the app the user launched from. The close-confirm path in `desktop/src-tauri/src/serve.rs` raises a window by the same three calls, and so does a Window menu row (`show_window`, `desktop/src-tauri/src/main.rs`), where chan is already the active app. On Linux the same library skips `set_focus` while its own minimized flag is set, which clears only once the restore is processed, so a restored window may not take focus there either. Both are read from the library's source, not observed.

## Desired contract

1. A window chan raises (a second launch coming forward, a close confirmation, a Window menu row) ends up key with chan frontmost, whether it was minimized, hidden with the app, or behind another app.
2. No focus is taken when nothing asked for it.

## Boundaries

- The raise sequence those paths share, on macOS and on Linux. Windows only if the reading finds the same gap there.

## Acceptance

1. First the reading on chan-desktop (owner, macOS): minimize the only window, bring another app forward, start a second launch that reaches the guard (`CHAN_NO_DESKTOP_HANDOFF=1 /Applications/chan.app/Contents/MacOS/chan-desktop`), and record whether chan becomes frontmost. The same for a close confirmation on a minimized window.
2. If it does not: a fix (activating the app itself instead of relying on the window's focus call, or raising once the window reports restored), and the same steps pass ten times in a row.
3. If it does: the item closes with the reading recorded and a comment at the sequence saying why it holds.

## Built, 2026-11-12

The owner reversed the acceptance's order on 2026-11-12: the fix is built from a reading of the libraries, and he walks it on his Mac afterwards. Nothing here has run on macOS.

The reading (tao 0.37.2, tauri 2.12.4, tauri-runtime-wry 2.12.2): tao's `set_focus` does nothing on any platform while the window reads minimized or not visible. On macOS it is the only one of the three calls that activates the app, so a window restored from the Dock, or a window of a hidden app, is left with chan behind. On GTK tao queues the restore and clears its minimized flag only when GTK reports the new state, so a `set_focus` in the same burst is always skipped. On Windows the restore runs inline before the focus call and there is no gap.

The fix: one helper, `raise::raise_window` (`desktop/src-tauri/src/raise.rs`), used by the three callers the row names. It makes the same `unminimize`, `show` and `set_focus` calls as before and then settles per platform. On macOS it activates the app itself on the main thread (unhide without activation if the app is hidden, make the window key, activate), and does nothing for a window that is gone by then. On GTK, for a window that read minimized before the raise, it looks every 20 ms for at most 1 s off the main thread and focuses once the window no longer reads minimized. Windows is unchanged. Which window `come_forward` picks, what it answers, and the close-confirm flow are unchanged.

Proofs, in an isolated guest at the tip: fmt, clippy, rustdoc, the Windows-target clippy and a macOS-target clippy (through cargo-zigbuild; a type check, nothing linked or run on macOS) green; chan-desktop's tests green as a normal user (1,022); 17 mutations each red on their own test, among them a fourth caller, the deleted activation call and a swapped platform. A Linux reading under a window manager that really minimizes, a second launch on a minimized window, focused of 10 at base and at the tip: openbox 10 and 10 (it focuses any window that restores itself, which hides the gap); metacity with chan never clicked 0 and 10 (tao's guard, which the fix answers); metacity once chan had been clicked 0 and 0, minimized or behind another window (focus-stealing prevention weighing the request's old timestamp, which this change does not fix). An independent review accepted the change with fixes, all applied (`dev/v0129/reviews/review-minraise-1.md`).

Open: the owner's Mac walk (a second launch on a minimized, a hidden and a covered window, a close confirmation, a Window menu row, ten runs each, with the installed v0.128.0 as the base control), which also answers whether macOS 14 and later honors the activation for a launch by path. Not built, reported: a raise from a second launch started in a terminal gets no focus under a window manager that prevents focus stealing, at base and at the tip (a follow-up could carry the launch's startup id or activation token in `Activate`); `unbury_window`'s three raise sites and `open_about_window` in `main.rs` do not use the helper and share the defect. Not read: Wayland, native Windows.

## A hidden app, 2026-11-13

The owner's Mac walk of the fix (`dev/v0129/owner/reports/parts-abc-at-8a44e110e.md`, Part A; macOS 27.0.1, arm64, a build of `8a44e110e`) passed parts A, B, D, E and F, and part C, a second launch with Chan hidden as Cmd+H does, failed. With the owner's tiling window manager quit (runs `Cn1` to `Cn10`) Chan was frontmost in 9 of 10, and a new standalone terminal window was minted in 4 of 10 (`Cn1`, `Cn2`, `Cn5`, `Cn10`) where the window the app held should have been raised. The installed v0.128.0 in the same case (`C0-1` to `C0-10`) left Terminal in front in 6 of 10 and went from 1 window to 7 over the ten runs, so the defect is older than the fix above, which narrowed it and did not close it: that fix left which window `come_forward` picks as it was.

The cause, as the second fix's worker established it from the locked sources (tauri 2.12.4, tauri-runtime-wry 2.12.2, tao 0.37.2): `come_forward` unhides the app with `app.show()`, which posts to the event loop, and tao runs `[NSApp unhide:nil]` there before any of `forward_for`'s visibility reads, which queue behind it. So the unhide has returned before the windows are read, and AppKit puts a hidden app's windows back on screen some time after that: in some runs `is_visible` still reads false for every window, and the choice falls to opening a terminal (`Forward::OpenTerminal`). AppKit's own mechanism and the spread of its timing are not established. This corrects the first reading, from the walk's log and the code, which had the windows read before the unhide ran.

The fix, on `v0129/raise-a-hidden-app` from `fccb600b0` (`eccf95adb` in `single_instance.rs`, `last_window.rs` and `raise.rs`; `949bbb06b` a sentence of `desktop/design.md`): `come_forward` reads whether the app is hidden before it unhides and chooses, and for a hidden app the windows that show come from the desktop's own books, the workspace windows it holds less those it put away, and not from their visibility; the shared raise then unhides the app, makes the window key and activates. An app that is not hidden, which off macOS is always the case, chooses as before. The reopen of a Dock click or a second `open` reaches the same kind of choice through AppKit's `hasVisibleWindows` and is not changed.

Its proofs are Linux-side only, in an isolated guest at the branch's tip (`dev/v0129/evidence/hiddenraise/`): fmt, Linux clippy, the Windows-target clippy in the Makefile's form, the macOS-target clippy through cargo-zigbuild, rustdoc and 1,049 chan-desktop tests as a normal user, all green; eleven mutations red on their own tests against a green control; and a typo planted in the macOS arm red only under the macOS-target clippy. The check for the owner's Mac is `dev/v0129/owner/v0130-hidden-raise-mac-check.md`: H1, the walk's part C with the build's window and process counts and its own log line of what it chose, ten runs; H2, the only window minimized and the app hidden, ten runs; H3, no window at all, not hidden and hidden, where Chan keeps running on its About window since closing the last window quits it; H4, a reading of the reopen of a second `open`, which the fix does not change.

The owner first moved the case to the next version ("next version"), then took the fix into this release once he saw it was built: "hold on, if the hidden-app fix is here let's merge it", and "i missed the point that the fix was already coming, there's no reason to not merge it other than "it'd take time to build" but it's built already so be it". It was merged after an independent review; the release report names the merge and the review.

Open: whether the reopen of a Dock click or a second `open` on a hidden app should choose from the same books, if part H4 shows it opening a terminal beside a window Chan holds; the fix's worker recommends that it should.
