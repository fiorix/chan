## What was seen

Lines at `e2a7e608f`, under `web/packages/workspace-app/src/`. A live drawing's canvas pushes a changed grid or background through its session with the elements (`pushDeltas`, `editor/ExcalidrawCanvas.svelte:280-310`). Four ways leave such a change with no authority that took it:

- **Refused while the session is degraded with its socket open** (`:479`), where a window stands that resolved a conflict through a save: its save waited on an authority that could not flush, timed out and degraded the session, and the resolution's snapshot then lands on that socket (`review-Frontend-17.md`, finding 7, read and not run).

## Desired contract

A grid or background that this window changed and no authority confirmed survives a reattach: after the snapshot it is still on the board and in the buffer, it is offered to the authority, and the tab reads unsaved until the authority confirms it. Which of the two stands when a peer changed the same value while this window was away is the owner's to rule.

## What to do

As a suggestion, the lead's shape: the session keeps this window's appState claim across a drop and a redial, and offers it after the reattach's snapshot has been applied, rather than handing it back to be replaced (`releaseUnaccepted` and `onSnapshot`, `state/sceneSync.svelte.ts`); the canvas's forget of the appState's baseline, which nothing reads before that adopt today, is built on or removed. The same claim covers a change refused while the socket was down, between a new socket's opening and its snapshot, and while degraded with the socket open. Red first: the mounted pin above, with its assertions turned to the pick on the board, in the buffer and on the new socket's push; today it shows the snapshot's.

`web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/sceneSync.svelte.ts` (`pushScene`, `releaseUnaccepted`, `onSnapshot`), `src/editor/ExcalidrawCanvas.svelte` (`applyRemote`, `forgetBroadcast`), their tests, and `src/editor/design.md:94`. The server's scene sessions are unchanged.

1. A grid or background refused while the socket is down, and one on the wire when it drops, is on the board and in the buffer after the reattach, and reaches the authority; pinned red first through the mounted pin above.

## What shipped

Built on 2026-10-03 on the v0.102.0 integration branch and not on `main`, in a range the lead accepted after two repairs, on its reports, its status files and an independent review of each part. No browser was driven: every effect is read in mounted tests over the demo transport and the drawing library's stand-in. This record was written that day from those.

The session keeps the appState keys this window changed as a claim, taken only from a board that adopted its session's scene and never from a seed (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/sceneSync.svelte.ts`). It lays the claim over the authority's appState on the board and in the push. So a grid or background picked while the window was reconnecting, as its connection dropped, or while its session had fallen back to plain saves stays on the board, is sent once the window has caught up, and stands over a change another window made to the same key, while a change to another key is kept. The claim ends at the ack of the push that carried it, at a stop, at any reload that takes the disk's scene and when the tab turns read only; it follows its tab through a rename.

A push's appState is the authority's with the claim's keys over it, where it was the board's four serializer keys, so the file the authority writes keeps keys the old push dropped. Left: two pushes that cross on the wire still end with the later one's whole appState; a queued push built from a stale shadow can overwrite a peer's key, which is ordered; a pick on a board that has not adopted its scene is replaced by the next snapshot.

A queued push was repaired on 2026-10-03, in a range the lead accepted on its report, its status files and an independent review of its whole diff: a push that waits behind one on the wire builds its appState as it goes out, over the settings the server then holds, so a collaborator's change to the other setting made in between is kept; an offer that joins a queued push adds to the keys it carries and drops none; and at an adopt the keys the board changed and has not offered stay on the board and are offered at its next flush, where the session would keep their offer as a claim (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/sceneSync.svelte.ts`, `web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/ExcalidrawCanvas.svelte`). A push on the wire still replaces a peer's key for one round trip. A pick made before a board has adopted the scene is still dropped: raised as [a-pick-made-before-a-board-adopts-the-scene-is-dropped](a-pick-made-before-a-board-adopts-the-scene-is-dropped.md).
