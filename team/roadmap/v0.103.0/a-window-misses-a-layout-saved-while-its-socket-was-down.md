# A window misses a layout a co-viewer saved while its socket was down

Status: accepted for remaining first-connect validation only; general reconnect layout recovery is not approved.

## Owner decision, 2026-10-06

Close only the first-connect acceptance once the instrumented check establishes it, alongside the first-save layout check. Handle avoidable socket gaps under the page reconnect repair. General outage recovery would need a separate extension that first preserves and resends unacknowledged local saves; refetching on every reconnect can lose local work. Keep the shipped first-connect-only boundary explicit.

## Owner ruling

On 2026-10-04 the owner was given three shapes and took the first. The first: the page reads its session back at the first ready of its event socket and at no later one. The second: the page reads it back at every ready, behind a guard that first sends again a layout the server never acknowledged, which changes the save path every window uses. The third: nothing changes in v0.102.0, the smoke check waits for the first window's socket, and the item carries to v0.103. What was put to the owner against a read at every ready: the page marks a layout as saved before its PUT is answered and drops a failure, so a read at a reconnect after an outage would apply the server's older layout over a split, a move or a close the user made during it. So the ruling is: read back at the first connect only, and the gap at a reconnect stays a cost, written under Boundaries. Nothing was ruled about building the second shape later.

## Desired contract

A layout a co-viewer saves between a window's boot read of the session and the first subscription of its event socket reaches the window when that socket first becomes ready, without waiting for a later frame. The window reads the blob back once, at the first ready of its event socket since the page loaded, by the path a `session_changed` frame takes and under that path's rules: a window that is discarded or not yet hydrated reads and applies nothing; a blob the window already carries is not applied and causes no save; a local save that waits in its delay is sent before the blob is read, so a change the window has not sent is not reverted; and the read sends no request of its own but its GET, so a window that found no blob when it loaded and holds no layout still sends no DELETE. A later ready of the socket reads nothing: a layout saved while the socket was down reaches the window with the next frame for its id. The first-save rule keeps its meaning: what the load found stays what the load found, and a layout the read applies counts as a layout the window applied.

## What to do

In `onWatchReady`, at the page's first ready of its watch socket and at no later one, arm the refetch a frame arms (`scheduleSessionSyncRefetch`), and add nothing beside it: no second read and no second apply path. The page already records whether its watch socket has opened before (`watchOpenedBefore`, 2313, read and set at 2290-2291, never reset), which is the record the first read keys on; a reload makes a new page and a new record. The 250 ms of the refetch, its coalescing with a frame's, the hydration and discard guard of `applyRemoteSessionBlob`, its flush of a pending save, its echo compare and `applyRemoteSessionLayout` then serve the first ready as they serve a frame. A read made after the socket reports open leaves no gap of its own: the server subscribes the socket before it answers the upgrade and writes a blob before it broadcasts, so a save is either broadcast to the subscribed socket or already stored when the read runs. The refetch's delay and the save's 750 ms stay as they are.

What the read must not do: apply anything to a discarded window; apply again the blob this window last sent; drop or overwrite a local save that waits in its delay; send a DELETE the window would not otherwise send; run again at a later ready. The DELETE holds because the read sends only what a flushed pending save would have sent at the end of its delay, which the first-save rule and the follower rule decide as before. The first-save flag is not written by the read: an applied layout is recorded by the save snapshot and a kept one by the diverged branch of `applyRemoteSessionLayout`, as after a frame, and a blob that is refused or carries no layout leaves a window that found no blob holding nothing and silent.

## Boundaries

The change stays in the page: `onWatchReady` in `web/packages/workspace-app/src/state/store.svelte.ts`, its tests and one sentence of `web/packages/workspace-app/src/design.md`. The frame's path (`onSessionChangedFrame`, `scheduleSessionSyncRefetch`, `applyRemoteSessionBlob`, `applyRemoteSessionLayout`), the save (`commitSessionSave`, the exit flush, the first-save rule, the follower rule, a deliberate discard) and both delays stay as they are. The server's routes, its bus and the frame stay as they are: nothing is parked or replayed for a socket.

The read costs one GET of the session per page, at its first ready, beside the roster, health and extension reads that ready already makes.

The cost the owner accepted: the gap at a reconnect. A loaded page's socket closes and reopens at a network blip, at a laptop's sleep, at a tunnel's reconnect, and each time its tab returns to visible or the machine wakes. A layout a co-viewer saves in such a span reaches the window only with the next frame for its id; until then the window shows the older layout, and its next save sends that older layout over the co-viewer's. Closing the gap needs the save path to know whether its last save was acknowledged, since `commitSessionSave` records a layout as sent (3360) before its PUT (3368) and drops the PUT's failure: that is the second shape of the ruling.

Not covered. The first read is not retried and no later ready repeats it, so a server that accepts the socket while its routes still answer an error (the case `healthInstanceWithRetry`, 2330-2340, is written for) leaves the gap open until the next frame. A `deleted` frame missed before the first ready is not replayed: the read finds no blob and changes nothing, and a save of this window that waits in its delay then writes the blob again, as it would without the read. A frame the bus skips for a socket that lags (`ws.rs` 505-507) is lost with the socket up.

At the first connect the read helps a window whose first save is silent or not yet armed, which is the case the smoke check exercises. A window that restored a layout sends that layout at its first save whether or not it changed, since the save snapshot starts unset (3235) and no boot path seeds it, and the read sends a waiting save before it reads (3440-3444): that routine PUT goes out first and replaces a co-viewer's newer blob, as it does without the read 750 ms after the boot, and the co-viewer's change is then lost in both windows. A window that does not send again the layout it has just loaded is a different contract and is not built here.

The read also reaches a window whose boot chose another layout than the blob. A page opened with `?fresh=1` (`readAndConsumeFreshFlag`, 3096-3104, read by `bootstrap` at 2656 and by `bootstrapStandalone` at 2506) skips the blob at its boot. A workspace window so opened starts with one empty pane, its first save is silent, and the first ready's read then applies the blob, so the page shows the layout it was opened to skip. A standalone window so opened seeds a terminal (2567-2569), and its first save, when it waits at the read, is sent first and replaces the blob, as it does without the read. No producer of that marker was found in the tracked code. A window restored from a layout hash that differs from the blob (2668-2679) keeps the hash when its first save already waits at the read, since that save is sent first, and takes the blob when the read comes before the save is armed (`App.svelte` arms it once the app is bootstrapped, 271-277 and 431). A window whose boot read failed takes the blob at its first ready through the reconcile, which restores tabs and not the tree expansion.

The read cannot tell a blob newer than this window's layout from an older one. At a first connect that matters in one narrow case: a page whose socket first opens late, after an outage that began once its boot reads had answered, may have changed its layout in that span and had the save fail; its snapshot is then ahead of the server, and the first ready's read applies the server's older blob over the change. The same holds, for the length of one request, for a save still in flight when the read runs. A file tab with unsaved edits is kept, as on any apply.

The first read is one more start of a path that has its own item, [an-empty-window-deletes-a-peers-layout-it-cannot-attach](../done/an-empty-window-deletes-a-peers-layout-it-cannot-attach.md): a window whose load found a blob, and that holds nothing after a peer's layout it cannot attach, deletes that blob at its next save. A peer's frame starts the same path, and the fix is that item's.

Out of scope: the structural comparison in the `deleted` case of `onSessionChangedFrame`, which the first-save item records as a cost; Hybrid Nav's stale rule, which works once the blob is read; the smoke check's own waits.
