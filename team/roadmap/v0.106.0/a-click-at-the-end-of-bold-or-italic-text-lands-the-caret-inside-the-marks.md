# A click at the end of bold or italic text lands the caret inside the marks

Status: raised by the owner on 2026-10-12 while editing a Markdown file in the editor; written down, not reproduced by a team, not designed. Accepted into v0.106.0 on 2026-10-12 by the owner asking for it, at the round launch of that day: its first step (the reproduction named below) is accepted, and nothing past the decision it names. Reproduced with offsets on 2026-10-12 (see "Reproduction"); the repair's design is with the review seat.

## What was seen

The owner's words: "Clicking on a bold or italic markdown places the cursor *inside* the block, not outside... e.g. if I want to click the "**reply:**" my cursor currently lands "**reply<here>:**" instead of "**reply:**<here>"". The file was `dev/v0106-open-questions.md`, whose lines read `**Reply:**` with the colon inside the bold marks; typing after the click therefore went into the bold run, and by the owner's example the caret was a character short of the visible end as well.

Not read at source to a cause. The editor hides inline marks in its rendered mode (`web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/decorations/marks.ts`), and click placement passes through CodeMirror's hit test plus `editor/click_caret.ts` (which by its header acts only when the precise hit test misses) and `editor/caret_mapping.ts`. Not established: which of the two the owner saw (the caret before the last visible character, or after it and before the hidden closing marks, which look the same on screen once the marks are hidden); whether it needs the bold run to end the line; the same for italic, code and link text; whether a click in the blank space right of the line behaves differently from a click on the last glyph.

## Desired contract

A click at or past the visible end of bold, italic or other marked text puts the caret after the closing marks, so the next typed character is outside the run. A click inside the run still edits inside it.

## What to do

Reproduce in a guest's Chrome with the owner's line (`**Reply:**` ending a line, and the same mid-line), clicking on the last glyph, just right of it and in the row's blank space, and read the selection's document offset each time. Find where the offset is chosen and which side of the hidden marks it associates with. Then the repair, design to the reviewer first if it changes caret association for every hidden mark.

## Boundaries

`web/packages/workspace-app/src/editor/decorations/marks.ts`, `editor/click_caret.ts`, `editor/caret_mapping.ts` and their tests; a browser check under `scripts/e2e/browser-smoke/checks/` for the click. Not changed: keyboard caret motion through marks unless the reproduction shows the same fault there.

## Acceptance

1. The fault reproduced with document offsets for each click position, per mark kind tried.
2. A click on or right of the last glyph of a marked run lands after its closing marks, pinned by a test that is red before the repair on its own assertion, for bold and italic at least, at a line end and mid-line.
3. A click inside the run still lands inside it, pinned.
4. `make web-check` and the browser check alone are green in the owning guest.

## Reproduction 2026-10-12

By the frontend seat in its guest, in Chrome for Testing 155 at 1600x1000 against a debug `chan` and the web bundle at `549bf15cb`, with real pointer events and the expected offsets written before the runs: a Markdown file carrying the owner's `**Reply:**` at a line end and mid-line, and italic and inline-code equivalents; three clicks per case, on the last visible glyph's right edge, 3 px right of it, and in the row's blank space about 160 px farther right. Line end, every mark kind: all three clicks chose the offset after the visible colon and before the hidden closing marks (bold 22 for an expected 24, italic 49 for 50, code 73 for 74), so the next typed character goes inside the run; the owner's reading of the caret before the colon did not occur in this run. Mid-line: the glyph click landed inside the marks (bold 33 for 35, italic 58 for 59, code 82 for 83), the click just right of the glyph landed at or past the outer end (bold 35, italic 60, code 82), and the blank-space click at the line's end as expected. ArrowRight through each run reached the position after the closing marks, so keyboard motion does not show the fault. Every pre-click precise hit of `view.posAtCoords` was non-null, so `click_caret.ts`, which acts only when the precise hit misses, left the placement to CodeMirror's own hit test, and `caret_mapping.ts` maps images only; one mid-line right click's precise hit of 36 became a final offset of 35, so the precise hit is not the post-click selection. Three earlier driver attempts failed on the driver (a status toast under the click, a nested syntax span) and are kept. The seat's proposal, with the review seat before code: a pointer placement rule for a click at or right of the last visible glyph when the selected offset is left at a run's inner end, mapping it to the paired mark's outer end, leaving inner clicks, later text and keyboard motion on their current path. The lead's ruling on the seat's question: no owner-browser reproduction is needed before the repair; the measured fault is the contract's failure, and a caret landed after the closing marks covers the visible caret the owner described either way. Records: `reports/mdclick-measure-Frontend106.md`, `evidence/Frontend106/mdclick-browser-4.log` and `mdclick-measure.mjs` on the round's bus.

## Lead's decision on the design 2026-10-12

The frontend seat's proposal (`reports/mdclick-measure-Frontend106.md` in the round's bus: a pointer placement that leaves the selected offset at a run's inner end is mapped to the paired mark's outer end; inner clicks, later text and keyboard motion unchanged) is accepted with the review seat's six amendments (`reviews/review-Review106-mdclick-design-1.md`, part 1). The rule reads the selection the pointer's own transaction set, in a transaction filter on an empty `select.pointer` selection in `click_caret.ts`, through one pure function from offset to offset, with no glyph geometry and no reading ahead of the event; the helper's own blank-area dispatch goes through the same function. It applies only when the selection of the transaction's start state does not intersect the run's outer range, by the decoration's own predicate, imported, so a click between the last character and a visible closing mark stays where it lands, and a click at the outer end reveals the marks with the caret after them. The mousedown stays CodeMirror's, so a drag from the run's end still selects. The mapping repeats across nested runs and covers the four paired kinds through one definition of a run's ends exported from `marks.ts`. Links and autolinks hide a tail too and were not clicked: the seat measures a link, an autolink and a strikethrough row first, with one more arm, press, move the pointer one pixel, release, and read the selection, since the owner's caret was before the colon and a motionless click did not show that; the range covers what those rows show, and if the owner's exact position is not reproduced the item says so and why. The pins are the review's: in the gate, the function and the filter on the measurement's fixture and offsets (22 to 24, 33 to 35, 49 to 50, 58 to 59, 73 to 74, 82 to 83, one strikethrough, one nested case), each red before the repair on its own equality, with the unchanged cases pinned (a caret inside the run, a caret at the inner end with the marks shown, a pointer range from the inner end, a keyboard selection at the inner end) and one mutant per condition; in a browser arm, real clicks on the last glyph's right half and in the row's blank space for bold and italic at a line end and mid-line, an inside click as the control, and one typed character after each with the document asserted, red at the base.

By the frontend seat with the first measurement's driver extended, the expected offsets frozen before Chrome, the original eighteen clicks reproduced to the offset (`reports/mdclick-measure-Frontend106.md`, "Second measurement"; the 36-record reading beside it; `tasks/task-Frontend106-Lead106-9.md`): a click on the last visible glyph of a link's label rests before its hidden `](url)` tail (96 at a line end where the outer end is 119; 126 mid-line), and a click in the row's blank space after a line-end link rests inside that tail (98); an autolink rests before its hidden `>` (177) and a strikethrough run before its `~~` (186), the same shape as bold, italic and code. ## Lead's decision on the second design read 2026-10-13

The review seat (`reviews/review-Review106-mdclick-design-2.md`) read the second measurement against CodeMirror's own mouse handling at the pinned version: a press outside the selection selects at once and then selects again on every move with a button held, the position under the press is kept while each later selection reads the position under the pointer in the layout as it then is, and nothing marks the release, so the three motion records are the library working as written: the press puts the caret into the run, the marks appear, the text moves right under a pointer that has not moved, the next move reads two characters earlier and the range is made. Accepted, with the lead's decisions: the mapping covers any empty pointer selection at the start of or inside a hidden closing tail, for the paired kinds, external links and autolinks, through one exported definition of a node's tail that the three decoration handlers also take their closing ranges from (a link's tail by its shape; none for an internal or a malformed link); the reveal is deferred while the pointer is held, since the library exposes no pointer-down state to a decoration, by a state field set from a mousedown observer before the library's own handler selects and ended by every way the review lists (the document's mouseup, a move with no button, lost focus, a drag start, any non-pointer transaction, the view's destruction), and the deferral is walker-wide, the lead's ruling, because the fault is that text moves under a held pointer and a heading's prefix or a fence moves it the same way; the author reads each widget's mousedown handling for anything that relies on its source appearing before the release, with the browser suite's widget checks as the net. The earlier condition that the marks be hidden is restated to read the same selection the reveals follow (before the press while held, the state's otherwise), so a second pointer transaction of the same press is mapped again and not left inside; the walker's recompute gate takes that value's change so the release alone redraws. The click-like range heuristic is not the repair. The pins are the review's, with one more arm read at the base first: a press on a character inside a run, moved one pixel, released, which by the source gives a range as well. Not shown: that the owner's hand met this mechanism rather than another; the repair removes the mechanism that moves the head.
