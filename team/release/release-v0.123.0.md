# Release v0.123.0

Shipped 2026-11-03 from `0.123.0-ga`: a no-rc release by the owner's word ("Straight to GA") of the five Hybrid Nav commits built on the owner's local branch `hybrid-nav-tmux` over an old `main` (`762f95240`, inside v0.121.0), with two additions made during the cut: a repair of the Cachix build's runner disk, and `chan-desktop-bin`, two commits from another of the owner's sessions folded in by the owner's word ("Fold in, round 3"). One Claude session on the Linux host rebased the Hybrid Nav work onto `main` at the v0.122.0 record, gave it roadmap rows, reran its checks in an sdme guest, gated the result, built the GA commit `fa2d08205` and the commits on it, and ran the proofs on the owner's words. The tag names the documents commit at the top of `0.123.0-ga`.

## What shipped

- `hntmux`, Hybrid Nav fights tmux in a tmux window: in a window that shows a `tmux -CC` window, Hybrid Nav now stages what tmux will be asked for. A split shows a staged New tmux pane and starts no local shell; a divider move, by key or by a mouse drag, previews and sends nothing; Enter sends the resizes and then the splits one at a time, and tmux's next layout shows the result; Escape sends nothing. tmux's layouts, peer saves and terminal renames apply to the live tree at once and never stale the draft, the window-size ask waits for the mode to end, and a tmux pane offers no A/B flip, send-to-side, tile swap or tab staging (`8caa04691`, `26c46dc93`).
- `hnstagedname`, a terminal staged in Hybrid Nav turns the draft stale on a name another window holds: a staged terminal now takes the name the server proposes, and a settled name for a tab only the draft holds stales nothing (`1bc4ca95a`, with the comment correction `3a74bd1f4`).
- `hnshift`, Hybrid Nav's Shift resize step does not fire on a US layout: the step is one constant and the cheatsheet's Larger nudge row is gone (`ef4cbab28`).
- `chan-desktop-bin`, chan-desktop on the AUR builds from source: `chan-desktop-bin` installs the desktop prebuilt from a release asset, the `usr/` tree of a `chan-desktop` package built from the tag by the `chan-desktop` recipe on an Arch Linux Archive snapshot six weeks before the tagged commit; x86_64 only. Its asset upload and AUR push run in a chain of their own in `publish-downstream`, so a failure there holds back neither `chan` nor `chan-desktop` (`a65870f7e`, which moves the AUR publication steps into `publish-aur.sh`, and `08aa45220`).
- The Cachix build frees the runner's disk before its two Nix release builds (`c7967899d`); see Validation.

## Rebase and review

The branch's base sat 49 commits behind `main`, below v0.121.0's code fixes and v0.122.0's documents sweep. The five commits were cherry-picked one at a time onto `12b1da9d6` in a new worktree. The fourth and fifth applied clean. The first three conflicted in three files: `App.paneMode.test.ts`, where both sides edited the header comment and an import line and appended a `describe` block at the same place (both kept, the comment merged); the manual's tmux section, where both sides edited the section's tail (upstream's close paragraph and its new pointer kept, the Hybrid Nav paragraph inserted before them); and the workspace app's `design.md`, whose detailed tmux paragraphs the v0.122.0 rewrite had removed. There upstream's text was kept and the branch's additions were condensed into it: a sentence that a tmux window's panes have no sides, and a paragraph on what Hybrid Nav stages in a tmux window, keeping the behaviour and dropping the function names; the owner accepted that wording ("Accept condensed"). The rebase was read against both sides: `git range-diff` shows the fourth and fifth commits identical and the first three changed only at the conflicts; the fourteen paths only the branch changed equal the branch's files byte for byte; in the four shared files that merged by themselves (`App.svelte`, `Pane.svelte`, `tabs.svelte.ts`, `navigation.html`) the added and removed lines equal the branch's; and v0.121.0's changes in those files (the key guard under an open dialog, the tab drag's source on dragend, the window close's discard) were read as independent of Hybrid Nav's tmux path. A documents commit (`bb28ecfc6`) gave the work three roadmap rows.

The additions went on top of the GA commit rather than into it. `c7967899d` came from the Cachix dry run's red (Validation). `chan-desktop-bin` was committed by its session as two commits over `12b1da9d6` and cherry-picked onto `c7967899d` with no conflict: both picks carry the patch ids of the originals, its fourteen other paths equal its branch's files, and `publish-downstream.yml` carries both the disk step and the new chain. A read-only review of the series found no defect that fails or blocks the release or the other publication jobs, and named the risks below. A claim-by-claim check of the GA commit's documents, by a second read-only reader, found seven statements to correct in this report, two roadmap items and the changelog; the owner had them carried in this documents commit, which also adds `chan-desktop-bin`'s texts.

## Retrospective

Highlights: the rebase over a 49-commit gap and a documents rewrite kept every code line of the branch, and the branch's own browser checks and suite reran green in an isolated guest within minutes; a second reader caught two wrong statements in the release texts before the tag. Lowlights: the design document the branch edited no longer had the paragraphs it edited, so its additions were rewritten rather than replayed; the cut scripts reused from v0.122.0 were moved to this version by text substitution, which missed the backslash-escaped spellings, and one of them would have let the stale-pin check pass vacuously (the GA build's own path guard refused first); the Cachix build had no disk headroom against a runner image change and took two reds to show it; folding in a change during the cut cost a third round of proofs; the Windows cross-check is still refused on this host. Honest feedback: the tmux staging was checked in a browser against a real `tmux -CC`, not in chan-desktop, and `chan-desktop-bin`'s asset upload and AUR push run first at this tag.
