## Retrospective

Highlights: the team's five lanes built, read and gated within about two and a half hours of the launch, two of them rows the owner added mid-build; the owner's branch reviewed, served and gated at intake within an hour of its push, with one survey settling every finding; the five branches merged without a conflict once the owner chose a deck centering.

Lowlights: the deck centering was built twice, once by the team from the row and once by the owner's other work, and the team's was dropped; the host's full gate carries two non-product reds, the AppImage strip and an sdme contract step that flaked inside full runs, which cost a step runner and repeated proofs; running four gates and two web checks at once on the host produced three load reds in chan-server tests and two vitest timeouts that no range could reach.

Honest feedback: the brief kept the release out of the round and the owner added it at the intake; as v0.110.0 noted, a round that may end in a release should say so, so the cut's inputs are prepared while the last lanes build. A row the owner is also building elsewhere should be held for the owner rather than assigned to a seat.
