- `streamlag` and its wake fix, a terminal that falls behind a fast stream keeps it. A ghostty view acknowledges the raw position it has parsed, and the PTY reader waits when the slowest holding view is half the ring (1 MiB by default) or 512 events behind. ## Retrospective

Highlights: the stream row, carried since v0.130.0, landed once a measurement changed its design.
