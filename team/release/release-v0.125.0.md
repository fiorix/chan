# Release v0.125.0

Shipped 2026-11-07 from `0.125.0-ga`: the release commit (the pins moved from `0.125.0-rc2` to 0.125.0 in the ten manifests, the three lockfiles regenerated, both Nix hashes harvested again, the Fedora specs, the sixteen rows moved to `done/`, the CHANGELOG section, the release index line and this record) on the round's integration branch `v0125/int` over the final candidate `728d0b31a`. Each candidate is a pin commit on the integration branch moving twenty-six version sites in ten manifests, regenerating the three lockfiles and carrying both Nix hashes harvested on the host, gated in the isolated guest before its push, pushed as a branch of its own name at the same sha, and green on its six runs there: `ci.yml`, `gateway-ci.yml`, `freebsd.yml`, the `release.yml` dry run with `publish=false` and the `publish-downstream` docker and cachix dry runs. The integration branch `v0125/int` was cut from `main` at the v0.124.0 tag `279ce66f8` and took main's record of that release's proofs and publication, `30760f33f`, as its first merge (`eccec08e8`). Over that base it holds twenty-nine `--no-ff` merges of verified tips from nineteen branches and eleven commits made on the branch itself, seven adding or amending roadmap rows, one correcting the gateway README's upgrade sentence and three pinning the candidates; eighty-one non-merge commits in all since the tag. Sixteen roadmap rows shipped: twelve accepted on 2026-11-05 at the v0.124.0 close, two raised and accepted on 2026-11-06, one raised by the owner on 2026-11-06 from a Mac report during the first candidate, and one written during that candidate, by the owner's word, for the fix the integration proof had found; none was withdrawn or carried. One fix shipped with no row of its own: two test-only repairs of the chan-wireguard resolution tests.

The release opened on 2026-11-05 at the v0.124.0 close with nine items that version left, one the owner asked for and one its read-back found. The owner accepted WireGuard enrollment at gateway sign-in the same day, with its design decided and two questions open, both answered in the build: identity's discovery document carries a `wireguard` entry, and the redeem returns the gateway's host names with their tunnel addresses. The Windows desktop WireGuard run of v0.124.0 left three findings made rows by the owner's word: a wrong peer key read as a generic discovery error, the core's ERROR-level expiry line and WebView2's own 504 page. The third reading of the endpoint resolution change left two defects in the endpoint rule, shipped as they were by the owner's word and joined here with the rotating name the rule's design left open; a clean Windows 11 guest found the uninstaller's leftovers; two WireGuard e2e suites reached past their isolation; and the Escape fix for the terminal's menu left two menu findings. During the first candidate, on 2026-11-06, the owner raised from a Mac report that some chan windows come back see-through after a display sleep, the native title bar painted and the desktop showing through the transparent content area, which the unified log traced to a webview presenting no frame after the wake; it was accepted for the candidate cycle the same day.
