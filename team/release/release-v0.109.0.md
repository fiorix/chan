# Release v0.109.0

Shipped 2026-10-24 as the one GA commit on `0.109.0-ga` over the frozen commit `38b2ff134`, the tip of the integration branch `v0109/int`: 83 commits over the round's base `980864e24`, which is the v0.108.0 GA commit `50b4dbee5` with three roadmap documents commits above it (`b5b94c0dd` raising `focdnd`, `rpchrm` and `webdep` and joining the Tauri crates to `rcdesk`, `c45f60685` raising `edopen`, `980864e24` raising `pstdoc`). No release candidate was cut, by the owner's word for the version ("this will be the entire roadmap, and i want it delivered end-to-end with no rc, straight from local testing to ga"), so the GA commit carries the proof a candidate would have given before its tag: its own full gate, the Windows cross-check and the packaging isolation test, the Release dry run with publishing off, the two `publish-downstream` dry runs, `ci.yml` and Gateway CI. The version held fifty-four rows: the twenty-four accepted at its opening (nineteen carried from v0.108.0 and five raised before the build began) and thirty raised during the build. Fifty-one ship, two (`dlstal`, `tsznat`) close on a reading that needed no product change, and one (`gstcast`) is withdrawn by the owner's word; none is carried.

The version was built by a five-seat team in one round launched on 2026-10-21 at 10:25Z: a lead seat for dispatch, integration, the gate, the documents and the release; a frontend seat for the workspace app and the launcher; a runtime seat for the crates; a hygiene seat for packaging, the release workflow, the sdme drivers, the native readings and the browser suite; and an independent review seat that read proposed designs, code ranges and their evidence before integration, together with the readers in other lanes of a value, label, preference or selector a range redefined. The owner's two completed builds were reviewed after implementation, some repairs followed a stated ruling without a separate design read, and the consumer read missed the marketing mock the lowlights name. The seats ran Claude and Codex models; by the owner's word on 2026-10-22 every worker moved to Codex (the review seat to Astra, the frontend seat to Sol after its last item) and the lead from Claude Fable to Claude Opus; by his word on 2026-10-23 the lead returned to Claude Fable, and the frontend and runtime seats moved to Claude Opus for the last row's build and its gate. Each seat worked in its own git worktree and its own sdme guest in a team-owned btrfs pool with explicit CPU, memory and disk caps; the host checkout was for reading and coordination, and the three commands that reached it through a shell's heredoc expansion are in the lowlights. Accepted ranges landed on `v0109/int` as item or stage commits holding their code, tests, design text and row records (an item built in stages, or repaired after landing, has more than one commit), and the branch was pushed to `backup/v0.109/wip` after landings, 56 times in all.
