# Release v0.104.0

Status: GA 2026-10-09. One candidate: `0.104.0-rc0` (`84f831661`, the version pin on the accepted candidate `c7178af66`; its `publish=false` dry run was green in all fourteen jobs that ran, and the two downstream dry runs were green). After the candidate was built the owner added the `anim/night-sky` branch, two more empty-pane animations and tuned defaults, and ruled that the version needs no second candidate for it ("once we merge we dont even need rc1, we can go ga"): the range landed on top of the pin at `97095ec98`, and the GA commit follows it on `v0104/int`. The tree that ships therefore has no `publish=false` dry run of its own; the GA commit's own gate, browser run and `ci.yml` run are its checks before the tag. The owner's ten display readings were not taken at the cut.

The roadmap section opened on 2026-10-08 with ten items, from a reading of the v0.103.0 report's follow-ups and known gaps that the owner accepted that day as the starting scope. The owner added four during the round: the editor's recovery records and the drafts and pasted attachments, both moved out of the workspace tree; the Windows cross-check driver's limits, by the answer "Repair the driver" to the round's first attempt at that check; and, after the candidate, the night-sky animations. Of the fourteen items, thirteen closed as shipped, three of them in part, and one as withdrawn. The three in part are native windows across a devserver restart (the direct-connection repair shipped; the gateway path and the macOS and Windows engines are unobserved), the six Low findings of the v0.102 Rust review (each has a disposition; one is a recorded cost and one guard series ran under a stand-in), and the owner's display checklist (fixtures and steps exist; two readings have no team fixture and no reading was taken). The withdrawn one is the fix first found only on a deleted branch, which the triage found already on `main`. Nothing was carried to a later version.

## What shipped

### Library, server and devserver

- A stopped devserver releases the workspace host that its registry reload watcher, fd parker hook and installed launcher fallback previously kept alive. The watcher retains only the library and feed notify; the parker hook holds its shared state weakly; the installed fallback holds the host weakly while active requests and WebSockets retain it for their own lifetimes. After an ordinary devserver end, a bounded blocking task drops the outer host handle. The server test binary's bulk-transfer lanes near exit fell from 26 to its one shared lane.
- Editor recovery records now live in the workspace's chan-home sidecar instead of `.chan/editor-sessions/` in the served workspace. An old in-workspace record stays untouched and is no longer read; State reset retains sidecar records, while Everything reset and forget remove them. Metadata archives neither export nor import the records.
- New drafts and their images live in the chan-home sidecar, outside the workspace search index until promotion. Save to Workspace writes a plain file for a draft without companions or a directory named for the chosen stem when it has images, preserving the primary filename and relative links. Images pasted into ordinary documents live beside those documents. Existing in-workspace `.Drafts/` and `attachments/` directories remain ordinary user directories and are left untouched; the retired configuration keys have no effect, disappear on the next save and warn when a non-default value is loaded.

### Workspace app and launcher

- Eight empty-pane animations join the catalog: Tenfold Dahlia, Beaded Torus, Spiral Fountain, Twisting Swarm, Branching Wreath, Drifting Galaxy, Ninefold Lotus and Eightfold Coil. The frame-rate page gains a fragment family, and the animation tuner remains a development page outside the embedded bundle.
- Cosmic Bell and Segmented Torus join the catalog as WebGL2 animations, and ten animations receive tuned defaults; of the ten only Amber Recursion shipped before, and its base and dark-theme tone moves from 0.855 to 0.545. Both new entries carry the catalog's WebGL2 classification, so software and absent-context welcome choices exclude them.
- An empty-pane welcome waits two seconds before creating its animation canvas or drawing a frame, and a tab that arrives during the delay prevents that work. A named software WebGL renderer or missing WebGL2 context restricts the welcome's initial, saved and keyboard choices to its eleven 2D animations; an unidentified renderer keeps the full catalog by the owner's choice.
- A browser `cs upload` command opens the chooser immediately with live user activation; otherwise it presents one attended request that keeps the destination and opens the chooser when Choose files is clicked. The request appears as a card or Transfers row, keeps focus across those surfaces, and retains bounded replacement history. The help text no longer treats a zero command exit as proof that a chooser opened.
- ghostty-web now sends the enabled focus-in and focus-out reports to the pty, filters focus moves inside the terminal, and sends nothing with reporting disabled. An invisible reply to focus-out does not raise the unseen-output dot; a visible repaint or shell output does.
- The Drafts group and workspace graph show sidecar drafts without indexing their content; Save to Workspace promotes them through the workspace facade and follows the resulting primary file. Draft browser assertions also refuse encoded draft identities and compare a copied wrapper's root, path and id with its source.

### Desktop

- A devserver withholds its window list and new feed watches with the existing 503 until startup restore and tracked mount attempts settle, preventing a desktop from closing windows omitted by an incomplete startup set. On a direct connection the desktop treats that 503 as a declined round, then counts a continuing run of declines as dead after nine minutes without a frame. Delayed Linux WebKitGTK arms retained the original window and page while the feed was withheld; intentional-off and discard controls still closed theirs. The gateway path is not covered by an observation: a gateway connection's 503 still counts as a dead round, since the proxy answers 503 of its own, and a representative gateway stack could not be driven in a guest.

### Gate and browser suite

- Two `chan-server` tests now own their order: the terminal restart test drains the first redraw before asserting restart frames, and the extensions child starts its catalog on a new line under a one-thread harness. Web tests restore the page's storage objects under Node 26 and make two drawing helpers wait for their own events. These repairs change tests and fixtures, not product behavior.
- The six Low Rust findings have recorded dispositions: the unbounded second join is documented as a cost, three contracts are corrected in documentation, two guards have parallel and one-CPU series, and missing stale-handle and overlay-label legs are pinned.
- The Windows cross-check now uses explicit CPU, memory, job and in-guest time limits with its existing disk cap, keeps source and build output in a private capped guest, compiles as a non-root user, and propagates failures and cleanup errors. The corrected real attempt completed its Linux-hosted Windows GNU cross-compile at the frozen candidate; no Windows binary was executed there.

### Observed without a repair

- The deleted-branch save fix was already present on main by an equivalent patch; the live unresolved-save guards and their regressions remain, so the triage added no product code or new runtime replay.
- A startup capability mint can need its expected retry. Under xterm.js, a focus report with broadcast enabled can reach other group members, and a terminal activation can send an extra focus triplet; those focus observations predate this change by source comparison and the earlier capture.

### Partial

- The owner's display checklist has fixtures, exact steps and team runs, but none of the ten owner readings has been taken. The owner must supply the original drawing, hung deck, reading-5 deck, usual window arrangement, VM and engine for reading 10; readings 2 and 7 have no team fixture. Team native observations are Linux WebKitGTK only.

## Team and process

## Retrospective

- **The delayed arms reached the hazard.** The v0.103.0 fast arms kept their windows once each but never observed an incomplete feed; this round held the restore open first, counted three real omissions on Linux WebKitGTK, and only then designed the repair, whose post-repair arms kept the window through that interval.
- **A stand-in that accepts anything hides the real tool's grammar.** The Windows driver's two constructed proofs ran against a stub that took any container name; the one real attempt was refused on a name the stub had accepted. The correction's proof makes the stand-in refuse what sdme refuses.
- **The combined run found what the scoped gates missed.** The symlinked temporary directory arm was absent from the owning seats' gates but ran in the eight-arm combined validation of the drafts set, where the path comparison failed; the set was held, the test repaired first, and only the affected server arms were rerun, with the other six reused after an identity check.
- **The team's native display fixtures used Linux WebKitGTK, again.** The restart, hide and export fixtures ran on that engine on one box; the candidate's Release dry run separately ran a Windows headless health smoke. A representative gateway restart stack could not be driven in a guest, because the stock desktop trusts only its compiled-in roots and a cleartext loopback stack cannot satisfy the tenant-origin rules.
- **A recycle that did not happen cost two hours.** A lead restart through a wrapper failed with status 127 because the wrapper could not find `cs`; the seats were parked for it, and the round stood until the owner asked what it was waiting for. A restart's receipt is now read before anything depends on it.
- **A timed-out question is not an answer.** The guest Nix route failed at its first real build; the survey that asked the owner for the next route timed out, and the lead's own ruling to proceed on the host was refused by its session's permission check, rightly. The second survey was answered half an hour later: the host harvest, as the three previous versions did.
- **Small windows changed the work's cadence.** On Codex the runtime seat compacted about every half hour while carrying a growing uncommitted patch; the lead answered by ordering each green intermediate state committed as its own seam, with no measured change in the recycle rate, and orders to those seats became one decision each.
- **Scope arrived after the candidate.** The owner's two animations and tuned defaults landed on top of the pin and ship without a dry run of their tree, by the owner's ruling; the range is confined to the workspace app, was read commit by commit against the owner's originals, and its integration tip passed `make web-check` and whole `make ci-linux`; the GA commit requires its own gate, browser suite and CI run before tagging.
- **Reds were kept.** The loaded-suite reds beside their quiet greens, the refused Windows attempt, the first drafts composition, the fixture runner's status 91 and night-sky's first web check each stay on the record with the green that followed.

## Known gaps

A WebKit page with an available WebGL2 context but a masked renderer keeps the full catalog by the selection rule; that case was not observed on a GPU-less native engine.
- All ten owner readings remain untaken, with six owner inputs missing and two readings without a team fixture. The Linux fixture runs do not answer the owner's display questions.

- Drafts lack live co-editing and server-side crash recovery. A standalone draft with attachments has no standalone-mode folder-prompt unit test; browser search assertions do not cover form values, alt text or placeholders.
