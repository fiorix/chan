# Release v0.102.0

Status: GA 2026-10-05. Two candidates: `0.102.0-rc0` (`adf953f6c`; its dry run was green in every job but macOS validation, red on one test of this round whose fixture holds only where the temporary directory's path is canonical; superseded) and `0.102.0-rc1` (`22c1e8fc8`; the fixture repaired, its crate added to the gate's symlinked-temp target and two test pins, with no product line; its dry run was green in all fourteen jobs that ran: the release context, two validations and eleven builds; accepted by the owner on 2026-10-05 before the hand test of it was complete, what that test finds to be reported after the release). `main` stood untouched at `fc78a081f` through both candidates. The GA commit is the one that first carried this report; the release is tagged two commits after it, on a test-only repair of five time guards and the record of it here. The tag `v0.102.0` is on `cd53294cf`.

The roadmap section opened on 2026-09-27 for what the owner accepted for a version after v0.101.0. It held 69 rows when the team was provisioned on 2026-10-03 and 98 at the second candidate, as the owner added items from use and as builds and reviews raised more. The candidates' runs added two more, 100 in all. At the GA 84 closed as shipped and two as withdrawn, and fourteen moved to v0.103.0: nine on 2026-10-05 before the GA, two of them accepted and seven raised for a decision, and five at it by the owner's word that every row not cut moves, four with remaining work and one accepted measurement not taken. The candidate is 1,203 commits over `main` (1,206 after the v0.101.0 tag), with 449 files changed outside `team/`. What a user sees is a devserver whose start, off and forget answer a retry in place of an internal sentence and act on the workspace they name, a restart that holds no restored workspace behind a folder that does not answer, live drawings that keep a pick, a stroke and a collaborator's change, windows repaired by their own page's socket, and a PDF export that holds the images its pages show. The user-facing list is the changelog's v0.102.0 section; this report is the round's shape and its limits.

## What shipped

### Server, library and devserver

- A devserver start that a turn-off or a forget overtakes answers `workspace is still releasing; retry` and closes at most the workspace its own open mounted, without force. A start leaves the saved on and off state as it found it, and a forget that fails leaves the workspace off.
- A workspace that another chan process holds answers one 409 and one sentence on the launcher's and the devserver's add, on, delete and forget, where v0.101.0 answered 400, 409 or 500 with internal sentences.
- A folder that stops answering holds less: a restarting devserver serves each restored workspace once its own mount is done and reports ready within a minute, a close answers within about ten seconds and one asked over the control socket within fifteen, and a quit does not wait for the terminal pruner's save.
- A forget or a reset removes only its own registry row, and an off, an on and a forget act on the row they name where a path was relinked or two rows name one directory.
- A search that keeps many directories lists their children in one walk (its last pass over 1,000 directories on 200,000 notes went from 3.3 seconds to 50 milliseconds), the graph joins hardlinked paths across pages, an MCP `read_file` pages through a text file past its cap, and a window's record lists the sockets that hold it.

### `chan` and `cs`

- `chan workspace forget` stops when its host says the workspace is still releasing: it leaves the workspace registered and exits 75, where v0.101.0 unregistered it on disk and exited 0. `chan serve` no longer starts a standalone server that the same lock refuses.
- `chan devserver status`, `start`, `restart` and `join` print the launch URL, a terminal with secret masking on hides its token on a loopback or unspecified address, and an unset `terminal.secret_masking` means on in a control terminal and off elsewhere.
- `cs export` renders in the window of the terminal that runs it and names that window, ends with exit 124 at 90 seconds without a finished page or at fifteen minutes in all, and writes its PDF under the server's guard. `cs terminal write --submit` takes `muse`.

### Desktop, launcher and windows

- The command deck and the launcher repair a window by its own page's socket, where in v0.101.0 any live socket on the window's id kept a broken window from being repaired.
- The desktop waits out a gateway's first fifteen 404s while a tunnel comes back, serves a local window from the same loopback port at every launch, restores up to four workspaces at once, and keeps a window hidden across a reconnect when its devserver did not answer the hide.
- A page that never read, or cannot attach, its window's saved layout no longer deletes a co-viewer's, a browser window shown again loses its hidden cover, and a file one window creates, moves or deletes shows in the other windows of its workspace.

### Editor, drawings and export

- Live drawings: a save answers once the file holds it, a grid or background picked while disconnected is kept, Restore keeps what another window changed, a large drawing keeps live sync over a slow link, and a board that is not open writes no stale text over the drawing.
- A later write of a file no longer discards the unsaved changes kept for it, and a draft drawing whose text does not parse can be discarded.
- One rule for a backslash in a typed or dropped name: a name that holds one keeps it, and none is added.
- A PDF export holds the images its pages show, names the first image it cannot draw, and lays a deck's slides out as play does.
- A key in a 20,000-line paragraph took about 195 ms and takes about 105 ms in headless Chrome: one of three whole parses per key is gone, and the other two stay by the owner's ruling.

### Packaging, release and gate

- `make ci-macos` and `make pre-push` say whether the four pins of a case-only rename ran, and the macOS jobs of the CI and release workflows hand them a case-folding directory. From the second candidate on, `make test-symlink-tmpdir` runs `chan-workspace` too.
- The browser smoke suite gives each check a browser of its own, and its delay proxy delivers in the order of receipt. Its diagnosis, at `30484cbcd` on 2026-10-03, read nineteen of forty-seven checks failing (eighteen at the round's base `e11043bb1`), none on a fault a run assigned to the product; its state at the candidates is under Validation.
- The fd-store end-to-end suite prints no devserver token, and a PDF export's error does not show the window's token.
- The four items carried from v0.101.0 with remaining work are cut: the dedup seams, the frontend comment pass, the frontend review remainder, and the thread a hung root takes for a caller that gives up, with one written cost.

### Withdrawn

Two rows close with no build, each as a written cost by the owner's ruling of 2026-10-03 recorded in its file: an add refused at its bound that can register later, with a launcher's on alone that is restored off; and an open dispatched before a removal that can recreate the removed workspace's metadata directories.

## Retrospective

- **Forty-two green gates and a red first candidate.** rc0's macOS validation failed on a test of this round that had never run on macOS, and the gate target that runs suites under a symlinked temporary directory had never run that test's crate. The lead had told the owner the test passed on Linux that way; a reading corrected it and an experiment reproduced the macOS line on Linux.
- **The round's Windows tests first ran at the GA commit.** No CI run was made on a candidate, and the candidates' dry runs build the Windows packages without running tests, so `make ci-windows` first ran this round's code after the pin commit was cut. Its one red was a time guard and not the product, and it still cost the cut a repair commit and a second round of proofs.
- **A reader found what a builder's gate did not.** The fourth lead records four ranges in one afternoon with a regression only a review found, and the fifth five of thirteen, two of them from the lead's own rulings. A repair per finding did not always converge: the PDF export's range took five repairs, and the sync latch's drop was built and reviewed three times before the owner changed its shape.
- **The browser suite was repaired and is not proven.** A reading found a defect in its own delay proxy that a test with a clock of its own then showed, and whole runs went green. Five different checks were still red across the runs of the day of the cut, three rows could not be cut on it, and its stalls have no owner.
- **What the lead told the owner ran ahead of the files, and the leads recorded it against themselves.** Twice a review's narrow finding became a wider claim in a message to the owner. Twice in one day, by the ninth lead's journal, the owner was given a size from a map made without the source that mattered, and the answer changed with the size: the removal's selection race was missing from the first and sized at 34 commits in the second, against 48 by its design record. Twice on the day of the cut, by the lead's journal, the owner was given a cause for a red before a file had one; one was the suite's first two reds blamed on Rust jobs beside them, when those runs had held the Rust lock. The lead also told the owner that the two whole runs at the rc0 commit ran alone, having cut that order beside a Rust order without naming the lock; they ran under the web lock.
- **Seats stood still for reasons outside the code.** The guest's admission check refused every Rust job for about half an hour on 2026-10-03, the lead's seat was rate-limited from about 00:12Z to 05:38Z on 2026-10-04, a test hung under a mutation held the Rust lock for half an hour that morning, and every worker seat stood still from about 22:15Z to 02:01Z that night while the owner's window was away. No cause is established for the last.
