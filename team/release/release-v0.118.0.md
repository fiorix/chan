# Release v0.118.0

Shipped 2026-10-31 from `0.118.0-ga`: the release commit (the pins, lockfiles, hashes, specs and documents) over the round's eight landed commits and merges, with no release candidate. The branch was opened on 2026-10-31 at the v0.117.1 GA over `main` at the v0.117.1 publication record, and every merge into it was gated from the branch's own worktree before the next one.

Three kinds of work met in the round, all on 2026-10-31. Two branches built the day before by the owner's agents and left unmerged at the patch were read, their last work committed, and merged first: the tmux card for a devserver-fed connection with its transport-loss cleanup, and the tab drag over an extension tab. The owner then decided the three browser-suite rows by survey and asked for tmux control mode off by default, which the lead built on the branch while two Codex lanes, dispatched by the owner from the lead's briefs, built the local `cs tunnel` refusal and the browser suite's migration to the Files directory view with check 95 and check 170; a third Codex task on the second lane modelled the browser's tmux control tab in check 195. Each lane finished on its own, committed locally, and was merged by the lead after a read of its diff and ledger; the owner waived the macOS readings by eye ("no need for the macos reading, if i encounter an issue i will file a bug") and the ghostty idle-keys row carried for want of a capture the build host cannot take.

## What shipped

- `tmuxfed`, A tmux card from a devserver feed has no record and no actions: the desktop keeps the `tmux_connections` records of every devserver window-feed frame beside the frame's windows, one snapshot per watcher, merged with the local registry's records for the launcher and `GET /api/library/tmux`; a request for a fed connection goes to the devserver that owns it over the existing authenticated direct or gateway transport, so the card's new window, detach and selection end work with no change to the wire (`988857b0b`). A connection ends with its transport: a feed round that ends drops its tmux records and windows, a retired feed task cannot repopulate a disconnected or replaced connection, a control-script exit closes tmux windows and keeps ordinary windows for reconnect, a late native build cannot land, and on Unix the control session ends the connection when the foreground process group that printed the marker is gone (`8e86745c5`). Proved in the `chan-tmux-feed` sdme guest with real SSH, tmux and the native WebKitGTK desktop: the direct scenario with the release desktop, the gateway scenario through real TLS gateway services, and the transport-loss matrix 13 of 13 direct and 15 of 15 gateway, silent gateway loss detected in 85.7 s under the ~90 s keepalive.
- `extdrag`, A tab dragged over an extension tab finds no split edge: while a tab drag is in flight every pane body covers its extension frames with a transparent `::after` box, so the drag's `dragover` and `drop` reach the body's handlers in every engine; turning the frame's pointer events off held in Chrome only, since WebKit still hands the drag to such a frame (`da803aa0e`, `b10686e39`). Measured with a scripted pointer drag in WebKit 26.6 and Chromium 126, pinned by the Pane vitest.
- `tmuxoff`, tmux control mode is on by default though few use it: `terminal.tmux.enabled` defaults to false; a terminal with no stored value treats the marker as ordinary bytes and asks nothing, the Settings switch reads an absent table as off, the documents say off by default, and the tmux checks and scenarios turn the switch on in their own homes (`f37a31eff`).
- `tuntoast`, cs tunnel in a local desktop terminal shows its reason only in a toast and times out: `HostControl::hosts_tunnels` is a host policy both chan-desktop constructors turn off, and `handle_tunnel` refuses such a host before admission or any `tunnel_open` trigger with one Error line that says what the command is for, exit 1 and the status mark; the standalone refusal names a devserver only; the native pre-dial defence and the page's pill stay for the triggers a window still receives (`f9ade7d4b`). The new pins were red before the change; the whole library, server and shell suites and the reverse-tunnel e2e suite are green with it.
- `filesel`, Browser checks drive the Files tree the directory view replaced: one shared helper opens a Files tab with a real tab-strip click, enters a directory through the path field and acts on the named row, with readings that say what the listing holds; the twenty-three checks use it and keep their own subjects; `lib/drafts.mjs` reads Drafts in the Files tab's locations rail; check 170 models the inspector below the status pill (`faf5a4f5c`, `1e77ff93d`, `d6bdacf40`). In the Linux guest the whole suite went from 74 green and 29 red at the baseline to 100 green and 4 red, the four being the reds recorded at `main`.
- `chk95`, Browser check 95 clicks a launcher card the carousel keeps inert: `lib/carousel.mjs` brings the card forward through the deck and waits for the ring's motion before each click; check 90 takes the same barrier after adding a gateway (`c0d2475c6`). Red alone at the baseline with the identical failure line, green alone and in the whole suite at the fix.
- `tmuxbrw`, A browser client cannot open a tmux connection's control window: by the owner's decision a browser keeps the control terminal in its source tab while the connection runs; the `release_term` arm returns at once off the desktop instead of attempting an open the launch route refuses and reporting a window that never opened, and the two helpers that path alone used are gone (`ea9470a2d`). Check 195 models it: the source tab stays, no page shows the control window, and after Detach a marker only the retained shell can print comes back through Copy Scrollback (`30482a04c`). Green alone three times and in two whole runs in the guest.

## Retrospective

Highlights: a round that opened at a patch's GA and closed in a day, with seven rows landed and every merge gated on its own tip; two lanes briefed from the roadmap items finished without a poke, and their ledgers read as evidence rather than claims. The browser suite is whole again: twenty-five checks that proved nothing since v0.117.0 prove their subjects, and the suite's red set is the four known rows.

Lowlights: the lead's first conflict resolution landed conflict markers in a merge commit and had to be amended, and a gate ran once in the wrong checkout behind a dropped `cd` and was only caught by its test counts. Both were caught by reading numbers, not by a tool.

Honest feedback: a resolver's exit must gate the commit it feeds, and every build step takes its directory as an argument. A round whose rows are mostly checks and docs still deserves the whole gate on the tip before the tag; the scoped gates proved the merges, not the release.
