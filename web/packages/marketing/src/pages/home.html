01 / COMPUTERS

The Computers Window.

The Computers window gives you access to all your Chan windows, local and remote, and your tmux sessions. Open it from any Chan window with Cmd + P on macOS, or Ctrl + Shift + P on Linux, FreeBSD, and Windows.

02 / TMUX -CC

Your tmux, in Chan.

Run tmux -CC locally or over ssh. Each tmux window opens as a Chan window, with its terminal and panes and layout. Use Chan's shortcuts and hybrid nav to split and resize panes, create new windows. Manage tmux sessions from the Computers window.

tmux setup and controls →

LOCAL

$tmux -CC new -A -s dev{{copyButton}}

OVER ssh

$ssh -t user@host tmux -CC new -A -s dev{{copyButton}}

Enable tmux control mode in Terminal settings. Requires tmux 3.2+.

03 / CHAN DEVSERVER

Your tools, on any machine.

Install Chan on a remote machine and use Add devserver in Computers to configure access. Connect over ssh or Eternal Terminal, as in the examples above. Your terminals, files, and workspaces live on that machine, ready when you reconnect.
