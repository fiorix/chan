What is chan?

chan is a supercharged terminal emulator whose terminals live in a server process, not in the window, on your machine or on another one. Around the terminal it turns any directory into a workspace, and it is built in layers, each on the one before:

  • Terminal. Tabs and split panes in windows, scriptable from the shell with cs, with tmux control mode. A window can reload or hide and the shell keeps running.
  • Workspace. Any directory, with search, an editor, a file browser, a graph and agent teams beside your terminals. chan serve opens one in a browser.
  • Computers. Chan Desktop's screen of this machine, its workspaces, its standalone terminals and every window.
  • Remote. A devserver on another machine, reached over ssh or any TCP path you already have. Disconnecting leaves everything running there.
  • Gateway. A gateway you host publishes a devserver behind sign-in, to you and the people you share it with.

Concepts shows how the layers fit together and what each one needs. chan is free and open source; its design documents describe the same layers from the inside.
