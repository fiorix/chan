What you can do

  • Write a slide deck as one Markdown file, a page break between slides.
  • Preview the deck beside your work, or present it fullscreen.
  • Draw diagrams from text in mermaid fences, or by hand on an Excalidraw canvas.
  • Browse JSON as a tree, and edit CSV and TSV as a table.
  • Export a document or a deck to PDF, diagrams and images included.

Preview and present

Markdown needs no preview: headings, links, images, tables, and diagrams render while you edit. A slide deck adds two views, also offered as buttons above its outline.

Slide decks

  1. Start a deck

    New slide deck, in the pane menu, opens a new deck as a draft, ready at # Slide 1. Or give any Markdown file this frontmatter:

    ---
chan:
  kind: slides
---

  2. Split it into slides

    Type @pagebreak, or @break, and press Space or Enter. The line becomes the page break, <hr class="chan-page-break"> alone on a line, which starts the next slide.

  3. Set its shape, if you like

    The slides: block is optional, and so is each field:

    ---
chan:
  kind: slides
  slides:
    aspect_ratio: "16:9"
    zoom_factor: 2
---
    FieldTakesDefault
    aspect_ratio"16:9" or "4:3""16:9"
    zoom_factorA number that scales the slide content, or a percentage such as "150%"2

$cs export notes/design.md{{copyButton}}
$cs export decks/kickoff.md --out out/kickoff.pdf{{copyButton}}
SourcePDF layout
A documentA4 portrait pages with 0.65 inch margins. A page break forces a new page.
A slide deckOne slide per A4 landscape page, laid out at 1920 by 1080, or 1440 by 1080 for 4:3. A slide that overflows is scaled down whole.

Images, Mermaid diagrams, and Excalidraw embeds render into the PDF as the editor shows them.

Details and limits
  • @pagebreak expands only as you type in the rendered editor. Written into a file any other way it stays text, and only the <hr> line splits slides.
  • Any aspect_ratio other than "16:9" or "4:3" makes the file a document, not a deck.
  • In the rendered editor, Cmd+Enter first opens the calendar of a date pill at the caret, or leaves a fenced block, before it previews the deck.
  • Present slide deck makes Chan Desktop's window fullscreen; a browser may refuse fullscreen and play the deck in the page.
  • cs export renders in an open workspace window: the terminal's own, else the one that joined last. With no window open it refuses.
  • A standalone terminal refuses cs export; it needs a workspace window.
  • On a devserver, cs export writes the PDF there; cs download brings it to your machine. See Transfers.
  • cs export replaces an existing file at the output path without asking.
  • It gives up, with exit status 124, after 90 seconds without a finished page or 15 minutes in all.
