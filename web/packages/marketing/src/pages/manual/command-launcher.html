What you can do

  • Run any command from the keyboard. Each row shows its shortcut, so the list also teaches them.
  • Search commands, settings, and windows at once, with words in any order: dark finds Theme: Dark.
  • Change a setting in place. An on/off row or an option applies at once and leaves the launcher open.
  • Reach any window on this machine or a devserver without opening the Computers screen.
  • See why a command cannot run where you are: it is listed last, dimmed, with the reason.

The opening list is in sections: the commands you ran last, the active app's commands, then New tab, Tab, Pane, Window, Workspace, Settings, and Computers. Type to search all of them.

Scopes

Press Tab, or click a scope beside the search field, to see one part in full.

ScopeLists
Terminal, Editor, Files, Graph, or DashboardThe active app's commands. This scope comes first and follows the tab you are in.
PaneThe apps a new tab can open, then tab and pane actions.
Window, WorkspaceActions on the window and on the workspace it shows.
SettingsEvery setting under its section. An on/off setting or a choice changes in place; any other row opens Settings on that field.
ComputersEverything on the machine the window belongs to, this machine or a devserver: open a terminal window or a window of any of its workspaces, and focus, show, hide, or close any of its windows.
Details and limits
  • An on/off row such as Details shows its state.
  • In the Computers scope a workspace that is off is listed dimmed; turn it on from the Computers screen.
  • In Chan Desktop the Computers scope ends with All computers..., which shows the Computers screen.
  • A tmux -CC connection's windows are listed with the rest. Detach or end the connection from its card on the Computers screen.
  • The right-click menu of an empty pane, or of the empty stretch of a tab strip, also offers Reload window and, on the desktop, Open Inspector.
