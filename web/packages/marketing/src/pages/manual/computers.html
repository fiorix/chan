What you can do

  • See every window on this machine and your devservers, shown or hidden, in one place.
  • Open a standalone terminal, a new workspace, or another window of a workspace.
  • Turn workspaces on and off, and connect or disconnect devservers.
  • Hide a window while its terminals keep running, and show it again later.
  • Do all of it from the keyboard, one letter per action.

The cards

The screen is a carousel. Turn it with the arrows at its sides, the dots under it, or Left and Right.

CardWhat it holds
All windowsFirst. Every window on every machine, with its machine and workspace, shown or hidden.
This machineNew terminal and New workspace. Terminals lists the standalone terminals; Workspaces lists each workspace with its windows.
A devserverIts name and address, Connect or Disconnect, and New terminal while connected. Its terminals and workspaces are listed as on This machine. Click the name to edit its config.
A tmux connectionThe windows of a tmux -CC run, New tmux window and Detach. See tmux control mode.
Add devserverLast. The form that adds a devserver; see Connect from Chan Desktop.

A workspace row has New window, New terminal window, and a power button that turns the workspace on or off. Its arrow shows or folds its windows.

Show, hide and focus windows

Each window row has two buttons: focus brings the window to the front, and the eye shows or hides it. A row reads Terminal Window 2 or Window 1, and This window marks the window the screen is over.

Hide a window to get it out of the way while its work runs: start a build or an agent, hide its window, and check on it later. Close it when you are done with it.

Gateways

Press g, or click the screen's title, to flip to Gateways: the same carousel for the gateways you connect through, with Add gateway last. The devservers a gateway gives you show on Computers as cards that read via and the gateway's name.

Details and limits
  • On macOS, Cmd+P with no Chan Desktop window focused opens a standalone terminal instead.
  • On Windows and Linux the window's menu bar shows only while the screen is open.
  • The letters leave forms, a window name you are editing, dialogs and the command launcher alone. Shift or uppercase letters work the same; Cmd, Ctrl and Alt combinations keep their usual meaning.
  • Click a window's name to add your own text; the row then reads Terminal Window 2 [build].
  • A devserver's icon shows its state: connected, connecting, connection lost, or disconnected.
  • Select, in the top bar, puts a check on each workspace, devserver and tmux connection. The bar at the bottom turns the checked ones on or off, or removes them after you confirm.
  • The top bar's command button opens the command launcher with the Computers scope alone.
  • In a browser the cards stand one under another, a window row has Open window and the eye, and the letters do not apply. Closing a browser tab ends its window's terminals; hide the window first.
  • When several browser pages show one devserver's screen, only the session leader shows and hides windows. See Collaboration.
